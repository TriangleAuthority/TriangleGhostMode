# Game version 5.0: changes that affect porting

Observations from comparing the legacy game (1.32, Steam build 3280809, `witcher3.exe` 3.0.0.1259521) with version 5.0 (Steam build 25575366, `witcher3.exe` 5.0.0.1041720, released 2026-09-29 as the "Remastered" update). Per-file details are in [5.x-file-status.md](5.x-file-status.md).

**5.0 hotfix** (Steam build 25646871, `witcher3.exe` 5.0.0.1044392, installed 2026-10-01; the current `vanilla-5.x`): changes 10 files of the baseline. Scripts: meditation sound fixes (`commonMenu.ws`, `meditation.ws`), a marketing consent setting (`startupExperienceMenu.ws`), whitespace in four projectile scripts. Binary: `q604_caretaker.w2ent` (mesh, light and trigger channel flags) and a re-save of `monster_bies_combat.w2beh` and `monster_bies_constraints.w2beh` (no property changes). None of it touches what Ghost Mode changes in those files.

Anything marked *unverified* is inferred but not yet tested in game.

## Gameplay systems (from the official patch notes)

5.0 reworks several systems Ghost Mode overhauls. Check these before porting the matching Ghost Mode features:

- **Skills:** reorganized into a skill tree with three ranks per skill. New skills unlock through prerequisite skills instead of a fixed point cost. All skill points are reset. Skill bonuses, effects and synergies are rebalanced, and several skills are renamed. Ghost Mode reworked skills, perks and set bonuses extensively, so this is the largest overlap. New skill effect scripts include `avoidanceSignSkillBuff.ws`, `catalystSignSkillBuff.ws`, `convergenceTheorySignSkillBuff.ws`, `elementOfSurpriseSurvivalSkillBuff.ws`, `resonance.ws`, `overloadCooldown.ws` and `undyingSkillDebuff.ws`. Ghost Mode has its own `undyingSkillCooldown.ws`/`undyingSkillImmortal.ws` and sign cooldown effects.
- **Monster combat:** the patch notes say attack patterns, counterattacks, movement and effect application of all non-boss monsters were reworked; the game files show it in about 13 monster combat trees (fiend and chort, wolf, the gryphon family, wyvern and forktail, werewolf, arachas and endrega, ghoul, drowner, cyclops and ice giant, with small changes to the leshen, the Hearts of Stone boar, fugas and gargoyles, golems and elementals), while every other monster's tree, all of Blood and Wine's and the human trees are 1.32's (unverified in game; details in [features/ai.md](features/ai.md)). Ghost Mode changes monster stats, abilities and behaviour-tree tasks, including dodge, parry and hit reactions.
- **Finishers and movement:** new finisher animations, a quick-disengage move (hold dodge, press sprint) and faster climbing. Ghost Mode changed finishers and stamina costs.
- **Reforging ("transmog"):** equipment appearance can be changed at master craftsmen. New `transmogMenu.ws` and `guiTransmogInventoryComponent.ws`.
- **Looting:** loot from horseback, a grouped loot window, a loot feed HUD module (`hudModuleLootFeed.ws`) and item tooltips before pickup.
- **Throwing knives:** new `knifeProjectile.ws` and `throwingKnifeProjectile.ws`.
- **Aiming slow motion (from the code, verified 2026-10-15):** 1.32 slows time to ×0.6 half a second into aiming a bomb or a bolt (`PlayerAimingStateAiming.Aim` calls `SloMo`); 5.x dropped that call, so aiming a bomb no longer slows time at all, and the crossbow gets its slow motion from `rangedWeapon.ws` (`OnAddAimingSloMo`). Steady Aim (`alchemy_s9`) deepens a slow motion that never starts; Ghost Mode restores it for bombs (SKL-15).
- **Crossbow and bolts (from the code and data, 2026-10-25; unverified in game):** the bolt, crossbow and NPC bow data is 1.32's, and 5.x added no crossbow or bolt. A bolt or arrow counts as a headshot when the bone it hits has `head`, `neck` or `hroll` anywhere in its name (and `pelvis` on humans; 1.32: those exact names); a quick shot at a large monster with targetable parts (`HasVirtualControllerTarget`) flies at the targeted part's bone instead of `torso2`; a bolt shot while diving gets the underwater range and 60% speed, otherwise the full range (1.32: the full range while swimming, the underwater range otherwise; `W3BoltProjectile.ReleaseProjectiles2`); arrows derive from the new `W3DeflectableAdvancedProjectile`. Every advanced projectile (arrows, NPC bolts, monster projectiles) gains 2.5% of its damage per Geralt level each time it collides (`W3AdvancedProjectile.OnProjectileCollision`; 1.32's code, as legacy has it, has no such term): an archer's arrow (`arrow_01.w2ent`, 200) reads 205 on Geralt at level 1 and 350 at level 30, a crossbowman's bolt (`bolt_01.w2ent`, 600) 1,050 at level 30, and a reflected arrow, scaled at Geralt and again at the archer, 612.5 (**seen in game** 2026-10-25). See [features/xbow.md](features/xbow.md).
- **Enemies (from the code and data, 2026-10-27; unverified in game):** the monster, NPC and NPC-weapon data is 1.32's but 39 entries (the human combat templates' guard and counter waits randomised to 2–4 hits and counter chance 50–75%, `ConWitcher` 800 Vitality, allies' attack power, a few spiders' and boars' resistances); entity levels are 1.32's. The level code (`CNewNPC.AddLevelBonuses`) gives animals, rats and the djinn no level bonuses, puts guards 1–4 levels above Geralt (1.32: 11–13), comments out the High and Deadly bonuses and drops the deadly enemies' blindness immunity; `LEVEL_DIFF_HIGH`/`LEVEL_DIFF_DEADLY` are 10 and 20 (1.32: 6 and 15). NG+ multiplies NPC dodge and counter chances by 1.15 (`CBTTaskDodge`). The monster rework of the patch notes is in about 13 monster behaviour trees (see Monster combat above); the fiend's `monster_bies_logic.w2behtree` adds legacy's close-range melee branch itself, plus a new dodge, a hypnosis wind-up by difficulty and changed charge distances. 5.x's XML changes to the combat rolls are only `mon_bies_base` and `mon_wolf_base`; its new scripted behaviour tasks (drowner adds, three-state charge, summons with a level, teleport conditions, ...) are used by no tree or monster entity. `LEVEL_DIFF_HIGH`/`LEVEL_DIFF_DEADLY` are back to 6 and 15 in Ghost Mode (AI-01, DA4). New: `ConDefaultSilver` (silver-vulnerable humans), the succubi off the human template, the `mq1060` quest's witcher Reinald, a red-plague creature (`CAIRedPlagueDefaults`, `W3RedPlagueProjectile`). From the `mq1060` entity files (**verified** in game 2026-10-28, spawned): Reinald (`mq1060_witcher`, level 15) is his template `mq1060_Reinald` (a copy of 1.32's `ConDefault`) plus `Boss` and Gundar's quest ability `qmq2038_gundar`, holding the Netflix swords; the red-plague creature (`mq1060_evil_spirit`, alias `red_miasmal`, level 10, a group monster) is built from Skellige's Hym (`mon_him`) and the leshen (`mon_lessog_base`) with the Hym's weapon; the plague victims (level 14) use `ConDefaultSilver` with the thug and brigand combat templates. Kill experience (verified in game 2026-10-31, XP slice X3): Reinald's and the red-plague creature's entities are contract monsters (`W3MonsterHuntNPC`), and Reinald's sets `grantNoExperienceAfterKill` (no kill experience in 5.x either); Novigrad's community guard (`novigrad_guard_lvl2_community.w2ent`) spawns as a commoner (`ENGT_Commoner`, `ConDefault`) in unconscious mode. Monster nests' experience is the entity's (the base game's nekker nest: 20 every time it is destroyed, its boss 50; `addExpOnlyOnce` off), and the nest takes only a placed bomb (`interactionOnly`). 5.x gives NPC swords scabbards of its own (`bound_items`, e.g. `scabbard_nomansland_lvl2`). Entity files (from the files, 2026-10-28; the succubi **verified** in game): 5.x's Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine spiders are insectoids already (1.32: specters); 5.x renamed the quest succubi's abilities (`qmh303_suc` → `mh303_suc`, 550 Essence added and Vitality ×0; a new `sq205_suc`, 120 Essence) and rebuilt the crowd succubi (`succubus.w2ent`, `succubus_censored.w2ent`: one `stats` list with `sq205_suc`, hybrid category), which have no health stats at all (non-combat NPCs); 5.x's entity files are otherwise 1.32's but for re-saved data (drop-physics curves, sounds, meshes' flags). Area names in scripts are CNames (`'AN_NMLandNovigrad'`; `CCommonMapManager.GetCurrentArea()` returns a name, `GetWorldPathFromAreaType` takes one; 1.32: enum values). See [features/ai.md](features/ai.md).
- **Difficulty (from the code and data, 2026-10-29; unverified in game):** the four modes' enemy multipliers (`difficulty_Common*`, regular and NG+), the expansions' per-enemy difficulty variants, the prologue's attack-power cut (`PrologModifier`), Ciri's abilities and her cap at "Story and Sword!" (`GetSpawnDifficultyMode`) and the entities' fake level addons are 1.32's; 5.x adds no difficulty variant, also not for its new enemies. Changed: guards who knock Geralt out take 25/40/65/85% of his money by mode, at most 750/1,250/1,750/2,250 crowns (1.32: 25/50/75/100%, no cap). New: accessibility aids in their own menu, "Vitality Regeneration Boost" (`LowHPAutoHeal*`, read by `W3Effect_AutoVitalityRegen`, off by default) and the preset "Bard's Ballad: Focus & Rest"; a warning that lowering the difficulty may block achievements. **Verified** 2026-10-29 (DIFF slice D1, both trees): 5.x's multipliers read in game as 1.32's data says (health / damage ×0.5 / ×1, ×1.1 / ×1.4, ×1.2 / ×2.3, ×1.8 / ×3.3; Death March! +0.1 force, burning, will and shock resistance), a mode set through the game's path (`SetDifficultyLevel`, `OnDifficultyChanged`) swaps every NPC's difficulty ability at once and its maximum health follows. **The prologue's attack-power cut never applies** (1.32 and 5.x alike): `CNewNPC.OnSpawned` adds `PrologModifier` only when a local `playerLevel` is 1, and nothing sets that variable (0), so no enemy gets it (seen in game: none of six spawned enemies on "Just the Story!" or "Story and Sword!" at Geralt's level 1). **Per-enemy variants** (DIFF slice D2, 2026-10-29, both trees): a variant listed under a parent ability (`DifficultyModeAbility` tag) is swapped at once on a mode change, as the difficulty abilities; a variant in an entity's own ability list (the drunken master's `mon_EP2_hardcore_FF` in `ff701_master.w2ent`) is added only when the NPC spawns on its mode, and a later mode change removes it without adding it back (the engine's behaviour; the entity is 1.32's). **Ciri** (DIFF slice D3, 2026-10-29, both trees, read on a build without DIFF-06): as Ciri on "Death March!" the spawn mode is "Story and Sword!", so her enemies get ×1.1 / ×1.4 in 5.x's data (×1 / ×1 with DIFF-01's), plus the attack-power adjustment (+0.7 humans, +0.6 monsters, summed into the multiplier: a bandit's 1.07 becomes 1.77); her quest abilities are 1.32's. Her `OnSpawned` sets every NPC's difficulty ability and variants for that capped mode, and `OnPlayerChanged` (after her spawn on a player change, not at a save load) for the spawn mode. The journal knows only the entries of quests the save has reached (`GetEntryByString` returns NULL for the others). The native per-NPC update behind `UpdateStatsForDifficultyLevel` swaps the difficulty ability and variants but runs no level computation, and at a save load in her sections it does not reach the NPCs restored with the save, which keep the mode they were saved with (seen 2026-10-31, DIFF-09; the engine's, as in 1.32 as far as the code shows). The difficulty names are 1.32's ("Just the Story!", "Story and Sword!", "Blood and Broken Bones!", "Death March!"). See [features/diff.md](features/diff.md).
- **Experience and levels (from the code and data, 2026-10-31; unverified in game):** 1.32's: the experience curve and the level cap (`geralt_levelups.xml`, 100), 1 skill point per level in both trees, quest experience (`CR4Game.OnGiveReward`), the kill formula (`CNewNPC.CalculateExperiencePoints`), monster nest and dialogue-payment experience, the level-up abilities (`Lvl2`–`Lvl50`, NG+ to 100), every reward's experience and level and every quest level but 5.x's additions: 7 new rewards, 4 with experience (`q504_horse_returned`, `sq106_3_hammond_killed_crach`, the two `mq1060` priest rewards), one quest level (`mq1060_devils_pit`). Quest experience as 1.32's, read in game on a build without XP's slice X2 (2026-10-31, at the port's thresholds 6/15): on "Blood and Broken Bones!" a reward gives 90% whatever Geralt's level (a level-7, 200-XP reward 180 at Geralt 8), a Hearts of Stone reward 30 levels above him too (150 XP: 135), a reward 6 or more levels below him 5% (60 XP: 3), and half once `witcher3_game_finished` is above 1 (100). The game loads the DLCs' quest-level tables first and the base game's last ([development.md](development.md#csv)): seven tables, each through `CR4Game.LoadQuestLevels` (the base game's from `OnGameStarting`, the DLCs' from their quest mounters, `dlcQuestMounter.ws`), the same at every load; the journal, the quest tracker and the contracts' tracking rule (`CanTrackQuest`) read `questLevelsContainer`, and a quest no table names has no level and can always be tracked (read in game 2026-10-31, XP slice X4). The level-difference thresholds both rules read moved from 6/15 to 10/20; the new tree's tutorial (1076528) speaks of Ability Points per level. A level's skill point is given in `W3LevelManager.GainLevel` (`AddPoints(ESkillPoint, ...)` from the level definition), its level ability added after it by `AddPoints` through `W3PlayerWitcher.GetLevelupAbility`, and every level-up requests an autosave (`OnLevelGained`: `RequestAutoSave("level gained", false)`), which the game writes when its last save is old enough (one written in XP slice X5's scripted level-ups, 2026-10-31). **Skill mutagen slots** unlock by the total of skill points against their `unlockedAtLevel` (2, 9, 16, 28: `IsSkillMutagenSlotUnlocked` and the new character menu's `UpdateMutagens`), as skill slots do in 5.x; 1.32 compares Geralt's level (read in game 2026-10-31 on a build without the port's fix: at 1 point per level they open at levels 3, 10 and 17). **Who earns a kill's experience** (`CNewNPC.ShouldGiveExp`): 5.x added a branch, so a kill by any NPC friendly to Geralt within 30 m of him gives him the experience and the kill, guards included; 1.32 counts only his own kills and, within 20 m, a non-actor's, a party companion's or an Axii'd NPC's, never a guard's (**verified** 2026-10-31, XP audit F1: a friendly renegade's and a friendly guard's drowner kills 6 and 8 m away each gave Geralt +5). **The new character menu's locked-slot tooltips** say "Unlocked at N Ability Points acquired." (`skill_slot_unlock_description`), filled with the slot's `unlockedAtLevel`; the classic menu keeps 1.32's "Slot unlocked at level: N" (1070951, 1070952). See [features/xp.md](features/xp.md).
- **Prices (measured by script 2026-11-01, ECON slice E1; verified):** a merchant's prices come from the native price functions on his inventory (`GetItemPriceModified`, `GetInventoryItemPriceModified`, `GetItemPriceCrafting`, ...) with `economy_tagmodifiers.csv`'s factors for **his type and region, which are properties of his entity** (not entity tags, not where Geralt is: Novigrad merchants spawned in White Orchard price as Novigrad's; the generic Novigrad weaponsmith is a blacksmith, `type_blacksmith`). In float, checked against 206 item readings (buy, sale and three fees each) at ten spawned merchants (six types, Novigrad, Skellige, No Man's Land) under 5.x's and legacy's tables, with no exception:
  - **list** L: the item's price in the merchant's inventory: Geralt's own list price × `mod_legendary`'s factor for the merchant's type when the item has the tag (truncated: 541 × 1.2 = 649), for gear: a crafting schematic tagged `mod_legendary` does not get it (ECON slice E5, verified: Netflix Armor schematic 2, list 1,031, asks ceil(1,031 × 1.1 × 1.05) = 1,191 at the Novigrad armorer; Netflix Armor 2, list 5,181, asks ceil(trunc(5,181 × 1.2) × 1.05) = 6,528). Geralt's own list price is the definition's `price` for most items; gear gets a share on top from its stats, which falls to half at 0 durability (Long Steel Sword 27 → 32, Bear Armor 1 455 → 541, the random Heavy armor 01 736 → 747–752); the share is **added**, not scaled with the definition's price (ECON slice E2, verified: with legacy's prices Long Steel Sword 57 → 62, Bear Armor 1 1,851 → 1,937); `mod_noprice` makes it 0;
  - **buy** (what he asks) = ceil(L × Π over the item's other `mod_*` tags (|type factor| × region factor)); `mod_nosale` counts as a trading tag (−1); a negative type factor counts as its absolute value here;
  - **sale** (what he pays Geralt) = trunc(buy × `mod_sale`'s type factor × its region factor): a share of his own asking price, not of the list price; negative, which the shop refuses, when any of the item's type factors is negative (he does not trade it). The bonus columns (`area_*_bonus`, `quest_bonus`, `bonus_mq7018_*`) did not apply at any merchant tested;
  - **bolts** (category `bolt`): buy and sale ÷ 10, integer (a bolt costs a tenth of the price its definition gives), also in Geralt's own inventory;
  - **fees** = ceil(L × (the service row's type × region factor)), the factor product in float first: crafting `mod_craftitem`, dismantling `mod_disassemble`, removing an upgrade `mod_removeupgrade`; **repair** (`GetItemPriceRepair`) per missing durability point ceil(Geralt's own list price × `mod_repair`'s type × region factors ÷ 100), the total that times the missing points; **adding a socket** (`GetItemPriceAddSlot`) ceil((293 + L × `mod_addslot`'s type × region factors) × (the item's sockets + 1)): the 293 is the same for every item, at every merchant and with either table, not a row of the table (both measured at ten merchants, legacy's and 5.x's table, ECON audit F4, 2026-11-01);
  - **funds modifier** (`GetFundsModifier`, which `SetupFunds` and `IncreaseFunds` multiply) = `mod_funds`'s type × region factor.
  - **the columns** a merchant prices by are his inventory component's tags (`type_*`, `area_*`; the shop menus read them the same way, `invComponent.HasTag`), not the entity's: the generic Novigrad weaponsmith's entity carries only `ShopkeeperEntity`, its inventory `type_blacksmith` and `area_novigrad`; the Hearts of Stone enchanter (`dlc\ep1\data\gameplay\community\shops_and_craftsmen\enchanter\enchanter_01_lvl_1.w2ent`) `type_enchanter` and `area_novigrad`. Blood and Wine's spawnable craftsman and merchant templates (`dlc\bob\data\characters\npc_entities\crowd_npc\bob_craftsman\`, `dlc\bob\data\gameplay\community\community_npcs\craftsmen\`) carry no columns and are no `W3MerchantNPC`; the merchants of Toussaint get theirs where the world places them. Setting a spawned merchant's inventory tags (`SetTags`) changes the columns its prices use at once (ECON audit F4, verified 2026-11-01).

  Example (5.x's table): a Long Steel Sword at the Novigrad weaponsmith: L 32, buy ceil(32 × 2.2 × 1.05) = 74, sale trunc(74 × 0.15 × 1.1) = 12, crafting ceil(32 × (0.35 × 1.1)) = 13. The Tracking Bolt XBOW measured: L 86, buy ceil(86 × 2.2 × 1.05) = 199, ÷ 10 = 19 a bolt; sale trunc(199 × 0.165) = 32, ÷ 10 = 3 (XBOW expected about 14 from 86 × 0.165, which misses the tenth and that the sale is a share of the buy price). Whether 1.32's native code is the same is unverified (it is presumed: 5.x changed no script around it).
- **Skills with no ability:** some 5.x skills have no `<ability>` entry for their ability name (e.g. Endure Pain `alchemy_s20`: its value is the effect `IgnorePainEffect`); a DLC that gives one values adds a new entry (no `on_conflict`).
- Also removed in 5.0: the drinking-game minigame, the old combat focus-mode scripts (`player/states/focusMode/*`, `focusModeCombat.ws`) and `combatManager.ws`.

## Mod support

- **Shared Imports is built in.** The 14 new `scripts/engine/C*.ws` files and the additions to `engine/components.ws` and `engine/environment.ws` are the community Shared Imports mod (v0.6), which Ghost Mode depends on. See [legacy-ghost-mode.md](legacy-ghost-mode.md#dependencies).
- The patch notes announce cross-platform mod support through mod.io in the main menu, plus REDkit improvements. New scripts: `gui/menus/modMenu.ws`, `gui/menus/modmenu_states/*`, `gui/data/ModPopupData.ws`, `systems/modHandlerSystem.ws`.
- **Local mods still load from `<game>\mods\<modName>\content\`** (folder names must start with `mod`), plus Steam Workshop subscriptions. All are enabled by default and load alphabetically; `Documents\The Witcher 3\mods.settings` overrides enabling and priority. The fresh install has no `mods` folder; create it. Source: REDkit wiki ([Managing mods](https://cdprojektred.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/W3REDkit/pages/36339714/Managing+mods)). **Verified** in game for scripts (pilot slice 1, 2026-10-01): a mod in `<game>\mods\modGhostMode\content\scripts\` with a `mods.settings` entry compiled and ran, including script annotations and a whole-file override of a vanilla script.
- **5.0 adds modding features that let mods change the game without replacing vanilla files** (REDkit [changelog](https://cdprojektred.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/W3REDkit/pages/12058625/Changelog) for 5.0.1042178, 29.09.2026). How the port uses them is in [development.md](development.md).
  - *Script annotations* (`@wrapMethod`, `@replaceMethod`, `@addMethod`, `@addField`, since patch 4.04) now also work on events, states and (per the changelog) native functions.
  - *Scope-based overrides*: a new script file re-declares a class with its vanilla header and adds or overrides members, including `default` values; merged at runtime. Mods can also add members to vanilla enums. **Both only work from a script blob** (verified, pilot slice 2). Syntax and rules in [development.md](development.md#scope-based-overrides-50).
  - *Fine-grained XML overrides*: a DLC can redefine existing XML entries, or single fields of them, with `on_conflict="replace"` (the changelog spells it `onConflict`, which the game ignores) or `"extend"` (the changelog says the default is an error on redefinition; for items the game ignores the DLC's entry and keeps vanilla's, with no error in the script log: **verified**, ECON audit, 2026-11-01). Previously DLC XML could only add entries.
  - *New DLC mounters* for conflict-free DLC mods that change base-game features.
  - *Script blobs*: a mod's scripts compiled to `content\precompiled.rsblob`. The game compiles blobs as mod code; when a mod has one, its loose `.ws` files are ignored. Loose scripts are still compiled together with the vanilla scripts, the legacy way (verified, pilot slices 1–2).
  - *`wcc_lite compilescripts`*: compiles the game's scripts plus a mod's from the command line. Used as the compile check in `tools/build_mod.py` (see [tools.md](tools.md)).
- **New script types** `map` and `set` (generic like `array`; methods `Clear`, `Size`, `Contains`, `Erase`, `TryGet`, `Key`), per the [mod.io guide](https://mod.io/g/the-witcher-3/r/what-does-the-remaster-mean-for-modding) (unverified here).
- **mod.io** (the in-game Mod Hub, also on consoles) validates every mod and update. Not available through it: mod menus, custom inputs, configuration mods (`.ini` edits) and injected code; mods need a JSON file (name, version, description, game version) and lowercase paths ([mod.io guide](https://mod.io/g/the-witcher-3/r/what-does-the-remaster-mean-for-modding)). Ghost Mode's options menu would rule it out there as it stands.
- Debug console: `DBGConsoleOn=true` in `<game>\bin\config\base\general.ini` (REDkit wiki).
- Menu configs in `bin\config\r4game\user_config_matrix\pc\` are now UTF-8 with BOM (10 files). There is no `dx11filelist.txt`/`dx12filelist.txt`: the game reads every XML in that folder, so a mod's menu file only has to be copied there (verified, pilot slice 5). Settings are saved in `Documents\The Witcher 3\dx12user.settings`; the legacy `user.settings` is no longer read. See [development.md](development.md#options-menu).
- Localization: a Ukrainian string table (`ua.w3strings`) was added. String tables moved to format version 164: the text is UTF-8 (obfuscated per byte), with offsets and lengths in bytes; legacy version 162 stored UTF-16. A mod's `content\<lang>.w3strings` is loaded without registration, and its strings override vanilla ones with the same id (verified, pilot slice 4). See [development.md](development.md#localization).

## Audio

- **The game runs Wwise 2023.1.19** (statically linked; `witcher3.exe` contains the SDK build paths `wwise_v2023.1.19`). All 740 banks in `content\content0\soundspc.cache` are bank version 150 (Wwise 2023.1). Legacy Ghost Mode's bank is version 88 (Wwise 2013.1/2013.2). Verified from the files; version-to-release mapping from [wwiser](https://github.com/bnnm/wwiser) (`wdefs.py`).
- **Mods ship banks in `content\soundspc.cache`, format version 2.** The game's sound caches moved from format version 1 (legacy: u32 fields) to version 2 (u64 fields, a 64-byte header with an FNV-1a 64 checksum); layout in [tools.md](tools.md#project-tools-tools) (`soundcache.py`). **Verified** (STAM-07, 2026-10-03): a version 2 cache in `mods\modGhostMode\content\` with a Wwise 2023.1 bank loads (`SoundIsBankLoaded` true) and plays, game parameters and `game_state` states included. Legacy version 1 caches were not tried.
- **Building banks for a mod needs Wwise 2023.1, not newer than 2023.1.19.** Install **2023.1.19.8928** with the Mastering Suite and Motion plugins through the Audiokinetic Launcher: that is the version of REDkit's audio project (`<REDkit>\assets\w3_audio\w3_audio.wproj`: `WwiseVersion="v2023.1.19" WwiseBuild="8928"`) and of the game's runtime. REDkit's editor says "The Wwise version must not be newer than 2023.1.19"; the REDkit wiki ([HOW-TO: Implement sounds and use Wwise](https://cdprojektred.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/W3REDkit/pages/6327994)) accepts any 2023.1.x. Wwise 2024.1 writes bank version 154, which the 2023.1 runtime would not load (inferred, not tested).
- **Packages:** **Authoring** only. It contains `WwiseConsole.exe` (`Authoring\x64\Release\bin\`), which REDkit calls to build banks. The SDK is for compiling a game against Wwise and is not needed.
- **Deployment platforms:** only **Windows** (Microsoft > Windows in the Launcher; the Visual Studio version does not matter, since no game code is built against the SDK). Banks for a PC mod are generated for the `Windows` platform ("Generate SoundBank(s) for current platform", or REDkit's `WwiseConsole ... --platform`). `w3_audio.wproj` also lists Linux, the consoles and others; those need not be installed (console platforms need an NDA anyway), and Wwise may warn that they are missing (unverified here).
- **REDkit does not ship Wwise.** It runs an installed `WwiseConsole.exe generate-soundbank` and writes `soundspc.cache`. It looks in `%ProgramFiles(x86)%\Audiokinetic\Wwise*\Authoring\x64\Release\bin\`, then the PATH, then `<REDkit>\tools\wwise\...`; Tools > Sound > Set Wwise path sets it by hand. Modders add sounds to the `_Mod` bank of the `w3_audio` project and reload banks with Tools > Sound > Reload soundbanks (wiki; unverified here).
- **Licence:** the REDkit wiki says a licence is needed above 250 sound files; the free key comes from the "Witcher 3 REDkit Modding" course at audiokinetic.com/school (3 months, renewable; wiki page [HOW-TO: Get a Wwise license](https://cdprojektred.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/W3REDkit/pages/6327958)). Ghost Mode's one bank is far below that limit.

|---|---|---|
| Executable | `bin\x64\witcher3.exe` (DX11) | `bin\x64_dx12\witcher3.exe` (DX12 only) |

| Scripts | `content\content0\scripts` (1,491 files) | Same place (1,527 files) |

Depot paths inside the bundles are unchanged: DLC files still live under `dlc\<name>\data\...`, matching the mod's `files/dlc/...` paths.

## Bundle format

Both versions use the `POTATO70` magic and store the table of contents uncompressed after a 0x20-byte header.

| | Legacy | 5.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Header | u32 size, u32 total uncompressed size, u32 TOC size, then constants | u64 bundle size, u32 TOC size, u16 version = 5, u32 data start |
| TOC entry | 0x140 bytes | 0x130 bytes: name[0x100], hash[16], u64 offset, u32 size, u32 compressed size, u32 CRC, u32 compression, 8 zero bytes |
| Payload alignment | 4 KiB | none (payloads follow the TOC) |
| Compression | none, zlib, Doboz, LZ4, LZ4HC | none and zlib only |

In 5.0, about 40,000 paths appear in more than one bundle. For every file in our baseline the duplicates are byte-identical, so load order does not matter. `tools/extract_vanilla.py` handles both formats (see [tools.md](tools.md)).

## Encodings

5.0 moved from UTF-16 to UTF-8. See the table in [repository.md](repository.md#encodings-and-line-endings). Details:

- **XML:** all UTF-8 with BOM and CRLF. Quirks: `dlc/bob/.../items/def_loot_shops.xml` and `items_plus/def_loot_shops.xml` still declare `encoding="UTF16"` although they are UTF-8. 18 files have a tab in the XML declaration and 4 use lower-case `utf-8`.
- **Scripts:** UTF-8 without BOM. 543 use CRLF only; the other 984 mix CRLF and LF.
- **CSV:** mostly ASCII with CRLF. Still UTF-16 LE: `gameplay/globals/credits.csv`, `credits_ep1.csv`, `credits_ep2.csv`, `credits_ng.csv`, `credits_tw3re.csv`, `eula.csv`, `gameworlds.csv`. UTF-8 with BOM: `credits_ly.csv`. UTF-8 without BOM: `game/photomode_characters.csv`, `game/photomode_props.csv`. None of these are Ghost Mode files.
- **Ported files** should match 5.0: XML in UTF-8 with BOM, scripts in UTF-8 without BOM, CSV as the 5.0 original. Merging with `vanilla-5.x` gives this automatically for files where Ghost Mode did not edit line 1. Whether 5.0 also accepts UTF-16 or BOM-less XML is *unverified*.

## File structure changes (baseline file set)

Compared with legacy, ignoring encoding and line endings:

- **Scripts:** 71 added, 35 deleted, 341 with real code changes, 1,115 changed only in whitespace or the license header (`®` became `©`, trailing spaces, blank lines removed). Real changes cluster in `game/gameplay` (83), `game/gui` (77), `game/behavior_tree` (41), `game/quests` (36) and `game/player` (29). For example, `playerWitcher.ws` gained a skill-combo system (`SSkillComboData`, `ESkillComboEntityKind`) and grew from about 11,000 to 13,000 lines. `geralt_skills.xml` also changed heavily.
- **XML:** 28 added (new item sets such as `w3r_items.xml`, `dlc18_netflix_*`, `def_item_*_dol_blathanna.xml`, `def_gwint_decks.xml`, weapon scabbards), 1 deleted (`gameplay/abilities/geralt_mutation.xml`, not modified by Ghost Mode), 162 with real changes, 263 whitespace/BOM only.
- **CSV:** 11 added (photo mode, credits, `rewardsmap.csv`, ...), 15 with real changes, 70 identical.
- **Binary resources:** every `.w2ent`, `.journal`, `.w2scene`, `.w2phase`, `.w2beh`, `.w2behtree` and `.w2comm` in the set changed. For `.w2ent` the CR2W header version went from 163 to 164, so the mod's legacy-format resources cannot be dropped into 5.0 as-is.
