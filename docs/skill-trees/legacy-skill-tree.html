Bloodbathⓘ2 SP
mutation3 · mutagens 3/0/0
Each fatal blow of your sword triggers a finisher or dismemberment. Upon entering combat you immediately get a bonus to your Attack Power for each skill equipped: +10% for a combat skill, +5% for any other skill. ⓘ attack_power 0.005 per ability; sword-path skill +20 abilities (+10%), other skill +10 (+5%); hit: -1 ability per 1% of max Vitality lost (-0.5%); kill: max +40 (+20%) and refill; cap 500 (250%). Mutated Skinⓘ3 SP
mutation5 · mutagens 2/0/3

ⓘ Damage taken (not DoT, with >= 1 adrenaline) becomes a DoT over 5 s x current adrenaline (max 15 s); adrenaline drains 1 pt / 5 s meanwhile; at 0 adrenaline the rest is dealt at once. ⓘ Replaces Second Life. Triggered by drinking the Mutation Catalyst in combat (not swimming, mounted or in the air); 90 s cooldown (Mutation11DebuffEffect 90; design doc says 120). Drains up to 3 hostile enemies within 30 m for 6 s: drain/s per enemy = max(1, (sword base damage + average AP% + average SI%) x adrenaline / enemies); heals the same total; immortal and a free Quen while active; ends with a 5 m impulse (40 shock damage, knockdown). Example: sword 400 + AP 100 + SI 0 at 3 adrenaline = 1500/s.
