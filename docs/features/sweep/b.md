# Sweep B: Ghost Mode's own changes outside `files/` (2026-11-02)

Part of the sweep that accounts for every remaining change of the 1.32 → legacy diff. **Batch B:** every unit whose origin is `GM`, whose path is not under `files/`, and which no row of `attribution.csv` claimed on master `d6062e97`: **72 units** (scripts, the localization tables and their readmes). Investigation only: nothing ported, no game run.

**Method.** The list is `units.csv` filtered by origin and path and matched against master's `attribution.csv` (first matching row wins, as `change_units.py` does). For each unit: its change in the GM commit (`legacy-GM~1` → `legacy-GM`), its callers on `legacy-GM`, the same unit on 1.32 (`master`) and `vanilla-5.x`, and its counterpart in 5.x-GM (`b3ec3609`, whose scripts outside `scripts/local/` are `vanilla-5.x`'s). Strings: each row's key (by name, or by key hash against every literal in the scripts and XML), the item or text that reads it, and the vanilla 5.x text from the installed game's string tables (`w3strings.py decode`, `content0`). Line numbers are each unit's first line (`legacy-GM` / 1.32 / 5.x); "—" means the unit does not exist there. All of it is from the code and data: **unverified** in game.

**Result.** All 72 units attributed: unattributed overall 332 → 260, batch 72 → 0.

## Gaps and new features

Each one is something a player of legacy had that the port lacks.

1. **Caretaker's Shovel lifesteal on monsters (SWD-03, gap).** In legacy, Geralt's Caretaker's Shovel heals him in proportion to the damage of each hit on any enemy. The port keeps 5.x's (and 1.32's) bug, which checks Geralt's health type instead of the victim's: the shovel heals on humans but nothing on Essence enemies, although its description says it does. Legacy: `r4Player.ws` `OnProcessActionPost` (removed) and `actor.ws` `OnTakeDamage` (lines 2647-2662). Port: one `@wrapMethod` or the fixed check; SWD's area.
2. **FIX-NEW1 Projectile collisions.** Enemy archers' arrows and monster projectiles hit trees, bushes, crates, debris, doors and fences instead of passing through them. Legacy: `CBTTaskShoot.ShootProjectile`, `CBTTaskProjectileAttack.Initialize`, and the same groups in `W3ArrowProjectile.OnProjectileCollision` (attributed to DEF-08 and others; also not ported). 5.x has only foliage, for bow shots. Area FIX.
3. **FIX-NEW2 Quest items released after their quest.** 108 keys, notes, letters, masks and trophies stay quest items in vanilla (undroppable, unsellable) long after their quest; legacy strips the tag when the inventory opens, once the quest is done or failed or the map pin is cleared (7 always). Includes the Umbra trophy (TRPH's hand-off) and the `QuestEP1` tag of `ep1_poi_12_note_b` (CRAFT's hand-off). Legacy: `gm_bugfixes.ws` `FixQuestItems`, `fixquestitems`, `CR4InventoryMenu.OnConfigUI`. Area FIX (or ITEM).
4. **FIX-NEW3 Family Issues checkpoints.** During the Bloody Baron's escort of the botchling ("Protect Baron from wraiths"), legacy saves a checkpoint at each quest update, so a death in the wraith fight does not throw the player back. Known side effect: reloading one hides the botchling until the destination. Legacy: `FamilyIssuesAutosave`, `CR4ScriptedHud.OnQuestUpdate`. Area FIX.
5. **FIX-NEW4 String fixes.** Polish: the Meteorite plate ingredient's name, missing from 5.x's Polish table. Russian: legacy's corrections of eight vanilla texts (Black Blood 1 to 3, Superior Full Moon, Enhanced Grapeshot, Superior Maribor Forest, the secondary-weapon category, the money bonus). On 5.x these should use the vanilla ids with their keys ([development.md](../../development.md#localization)); legacy added new ids with the vanilla key hash, which is unverified on 5.x. Area FIX (or UI).
6. **HORSE-NEW1 Horseback hits.** A sword hit from horseback no longer empties the enemy's stamina (vanilla drains 100), and its bonus follows the weapon's damage and the horse's speed, up to double at full gallop (vanilla: a flat direct-damage bonus). Legacy: `DealDamageToHostiles`. Belongs with DMG's HORSE hand-off (the horse-speed attack power bonus removed from `GetPowerStatValue`). New area HORSE (in the area table, not mapped yet).
7. **HORSE-NEW2 Horse Axii costs stamina.** Calming a panicking horse with Axii needs 50 stamina and drains 5 stamina a second while held; with Adrenaline Burst, adrenaline covers the shortfall (SKL's hand-off). Touches a perk, so both trees: classic `perk_10`, new tree `perk_42`. Area HORSE, with STAM's hand-off list.

## Owner questions

1. **Caretaker's Shovel (gap 1).** Port legacy's fix (heals on every enemy), or keep 5.x's bug (no heal on monsters)? **Recommendation:** port it. It is legacy's behaviour, and the description already promises it.
2. **Projectile collisions (FIX-NEW1).** Port legacy's collision groups for NPC projectiles and arrows, or keep 5.x's (foliage only, bow shots only)? **Recommendation:** port, as legacy. This is a few lines; check in game that arrows still reach Geralt behind thin cover (fences, foliage) as legacy intended.
3. **Quest items (FIX-NEW2).** Port legacy's list as it is, or drop it? **Recommendation:** port, after a script check that each of the 95 journal entries resolves on 5.x (`GetEntryByString`) and the 6 map pins exist. Weight and price of the released items are WGT's and ECON's, already ported.
4. **Family Issues checkpoints (FIX-NEW3).** Port (legacy, with its visual botchling bug), or drop? **Recommendation:** port. It is cheap and is legacy's; the known bug is legacy's too. Record it as legacy's known issue, not as a deviation.
5. **String fixes (FIX-NEW4).** Port the Polish name and the eight Russian corrections? **Recommendation:** port all nine, by vanilla id and key.
6. **Horse combat (HORSE-NEW1, HORSE-NEW2).** Map HORSE as a small area of its own, or fold it into STAM and DMG? **Recommendation:** a small HORSE area (two features plus DMG's and STAM's hand-offs), ported and tested in both trees for Adrenaline Burst.
