## [DEF-05 Parrying rules](def.md#def-05-parrying-rules)

- a parry without enough stamina of a parryable attack (STAM-03) staggers. Legacy added the stagger, but vanilla's `ProcessActionBuffs` applies no effect of a parried hit of a parryable attack, in legacy too, so it never applied: a defect against the changelist's partial parry (project owner, 2026-10-04).
- parrying an unarmed attacker with a weapon stays vanilla: the punch bounces off, no damage, no stamina. Legacy's code makes that parry fail (the attacker recoils, Geralt takes the punch and pays the parry); its comment describes the opposite case (an unarmed Geralt parrying a weapon), which vanilla's `FistFightCheck` already refuses, so the change looks like a misreading, and the changelist does not mention it (project owner, 2026-10-04).

## [DEF-06 Counters and partial counters](def.md#def-06-counters-and-partial-counters)

- 5.x's spear break is kept: countering a two-handed spear breaks it. Legacy removed the branch as "not working anyway", which 5.x fixed (project owner, 2026-10-04).
- a counter blocks all of the attack's effects. Vanilla's code, and legacy's, applied them although the hit dealt no damage: a defect against the changelist's "prevent all negative effects" (project owner, 2026-10-04).
- counters of attacks that cannot be parried do not count toward the counter achievement and Learning the Ropes (vanilla's `TestParryAndCounter`); legacy counted them (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).

## [DEF-07 Counter bonuses](def.md#def-07-counter-bonuses)

- the +25% crit goes to the player's light and heavy attacks, not to melee attacks that are neither (e.g. `attack_super_heavy`, `attack_speed_based`); legacy gave it to every melee attack action. The crit function is asked with neither light nor heavy for bolts, signs and other actions too, so the wrapper cannot tell the rest apart (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).
- a counter gets a light attack's attack power bonuses (Muscle Memory, the light-attack perks), as the changelist says; legacy's code gave it neither. Since SKL-03 ported D47 (2026-10-14), a counter gets Muscle Memory's attack power once, as every fast attack does. Counters stay distinguishable from light attacks, for later counter-only rules (project owner, 2026-10-04, revised 2026-10-11).

## [SKL-01 Skill system rules](skl.md#skl-01-skill-system-rules)

- the character reset is built on 5.x's own respec (`ResetCharacterDev` and `ResetMutationsDev`, what the Potion of Clearance and the Restoring Potion run) plus legacy's extras (greater mutagens spent on research returned, skill mutagens unequipped, toxicity 0); legacy deletes and rebuilds the level, ability and effect managers. The result for the player is the same, on the path the game itself uses (project owner, 2026-10-11).
- the character reset leaves gear and sword oils alone; legacy unequips every item, the horse's included, and removes the oils from both swords. A skill reset does not need it (project owner, 2026-10-11).
- the character reset is refused in combat (5.x cannot unequip a mutation there); legacy's runs at any time (project owner, 2026-10-12).
- every respec through `ResetCharacterDev` (the Potion of Clearance, `gmreset()`, 5.x's other callers) first empties every skill slot by its ID, locked slots and the fourth mutation slot included; legacy's Potion of Clearance left a skill in a locked slot socketed at level 0, so its bonuses stayed when it was later removed (legacy's own respec rebuilt the managers and had no such case) ([audit F28](#skl-audit-2026-10-16); confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16).
- the character menu unequips the skills in locked slots each time it opens, before it builds its data; legacy runs the check from the menu's `setMutationBonusMode` (on opening with the mutation system on, and on equipping a mutation). The same result for the player, and it also covers a character without the mutation system (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16).
