# Deviations from legacy

Generated by `python tools/deviations.py --write`; do not edit by hand. Each deviation is recorded in its feature entry's **Decisions** ([convention](../feature-mapping.md#legacy-behaviour-first)); this page collects them for reviews.

- Deviations: 24; not confirmed by the owner: 12; temporary: 6

## [DEF-01 Dodge resolution](def.md#def-01-dodge-resolution)

- until DEF-02 replaces it with the graze rule, 5.x's Fleet Footed handling of failed dodges stays: vanilla's branch in `W3PlayerWitcher.ReduceDamage`, damage reduced on a failed dodge and a full dodge at the top level (the classic `sword_s9` has one level); legacy had the graze rule instead (temporary, until DEF-02; project owner, 2026-10-03).

## [DEF-05 Parrying rules](def.md#def-05-parrying-rules)

- a parry without enough stamina of a parryable attack (STAM-03) staggers. Legacy added the stagger, but vanilla's `ProcessActionBuffs` applies no effect of a parried hit of a parryable attack, in legacy too, so it never applied: a defect against the changelist's partial parry (project owner, 2026-10-04).
- parrying an unarmed attacker with a weapon stays vanilla: the punch bounces off, no damage, no stamina. Legacy's code makes that parry fail (the attacker recoils, Geralt takes the punch and pays the parry); its comment describes the opposite case (an unarmed Geralt parrying a weapon), which vanilla's `FistFightCheck` already refuses, so the change looks like a misreading, and the changelist does not mention it (project owner, 2026-10-04).

## [DEF-06 Counters and partial counters](def.md#def-06-counters-and-partial-counters)

- 5.x's spear break is kept: countering a two-handed spear breaks it. Legacy removed the branch as "not working anyway", which 5.x fixed (project owner, 2026-10-04).
- a counter blocks all of the attack's effects. Vanilla's code, and legacy's, applied them although the hit dealt no damage: a defect against the changelist's "prevent all negative effects" (project owner, 2026-10-04).
- counters of attacks that cannot be parried do not count toward the counter achievement and Learning the Ropes (vanilla's `TestParryAndCounter`); legacy counted them (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).
- legacy's changes in this function to Counterattack's knockdown and Perk 21 (SKL), Mutation 8 and its place first in the repel order (MUT) and the Lynx set (ARM) are left as 5.x (temporary, until SKL, MUT and ARM are ported; porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).

## [DEF-07 Counter bonuses](def.md#def-07-counter-bonuses)

- the +25% crit goes to the player's light and heavy attacks, not to melee attacks that are neither (e.g. `attack_super_heavy`, `attack_speed_based`); legacy gave it to every melee attack action. The crit function is asked with neither light nor heavy for bolts, signs and other actions too, so the wrapper cannot tell the rest apart (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).
- Counterattack's crit chance and attack power (`sword_s11`) and Mutation 8 still read vanilla's "Geralt has recently countered" flag (his next attack, on anyone, until he attacks), where legacy reads the countered enemy's flag (temporary, until SKL and MUT; porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).
- a counter gets a light attack's attack power bonuses, both shares (Muscle Memory by tag and per level, the light-attack perks), as the changelist says; legacy's code gave it neither. Counters stay distinguishable from light attacks, for later counter-only rules (project owner, 2026-10-04).

## [DEF-08 Arrow parry](def.md#def-08-arrow-parry)

- Deflection reflects arrows from its second level, as 5.x (5.x cut it from three levels to two); legacy reflected from the first (temporary, until SKL maps Deflection; project owner, 2026-10-03).

## [SKL-03 Fast Attack branch](skl.md#skl-03-fast-attack-branch)

- Muscle Memory's and Strength Training's attack power counts once, with 5.x's 0.1 per level (+30% at level 3). Legacy's code counted it twice (+50% played where the text said +25%); counters get a light attack's attack power, once (DEF-07 gave them both shares) (project owner, 2026-10-04).

## [STAM-03 Stamina costs for every action](stam.md#stam-03-stamina-costs-for-every-action)

- legacy's moved charge points for normal casts are not ported: legacy charged Yrden and Quen at the throw, like the other signs; 5.x charges them when the trap or shield becomes active, which in game happens at the throw (all five measured charging once, at the throw). Only a cast interrupted between the throw and the active state would differ (porter's call, 2026-10-03, not confirmed by the owner).
- a horseback sword attack is paid in `SwordAttack.DoAttack`, which both swing paths run; legacy paid on the button release, which missed the quick swing (a legacy gap; porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).

## [STAM-04 Stamina regen](stam.md#stam-04-stamina-regen)

- with STAM-05's option on, 5.x's own armor calculation applies, not legacy's rework of it (which ignored the Blood and Wine glyphwords' regen and added regen per missing armor piece), and legacy's per-item `staminaRegen_armor_mod` values (e.g. heavy armor −0.10 → −0.30), which matter only with the option on, are not ported; they are attributed here (temporary, until ARM and the glyphwords; porter's call, 2026-10-02, not confirmed by the owner).
- the regen effect recomputes its value on every update, in and out of combat (legacy and vanilla: in combat only), so option changes apply at once (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).
- the character panel's out-of-combat regen line leaves out the armor modifier (legacy showed it), because the effect applies it in combat only (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).

## [STAM-05 Armor stamina regen penalties removed](stam.md#stam-05-armor-stamina-regen-penalties-removed)

- the tooltip row is not ported yet: it comes with Ghost Mode's other tooltip rows, to avoid a whole-file override of the CSV for one row; it is attributed here (temporary, until those rows are ported; porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).

## [STAM-07 Low-stamina heartbeat](stam.md#stam-07-low-stamina-heartbeat)

- legacy's presets for this options group and its `GMSignInstantCast` toggle are not ported, as with OPT-01's other groups (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).
- the playing flag is not saved (legacy saved it); a loaded game starts with it off and the next stamina change starts the sound. No save impact (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner).

## [STAM-08 NPC stamina](stam.md#stam-08-npc-stamina)

- false starts: some species' trees start an attack task and end it in the same frame, many times before a real attack (ghouls, bears). Legacy charged those two in `Main`; the port charges every attack at its start and refunds it, regen pause included, if its task ends in the frame it started. This covers every species whose tree does this; charging in `Main` would have made 16 attack task classes that replace `Main` free (project owner, 2026-10-03).
- `CBTTaskPerformParry.IsAvailable` requires the parry stamina at all times; legacy only within the activation window, the same in effect (accepted at the STAM audit; project owner, 2026-10-04).

## [STAM-09 Whirl and Rend stamina costs](stam.md#stam-09-whirl-and-rend-stamina-costs)

- at 3 levels the cost reduction reaches 30% (legacy: 60% over 5 levels), per SKL's per-level rule for 5.x's three-level skills (project owner, 2026-10-03).
- with both Whirls equipped, `GMWhirlSkill()` uses the classic one, where 5.x's `CR4Player` prefers `sword_s35` (accepted at the STAM audit; project owner, 2026-10-04).
