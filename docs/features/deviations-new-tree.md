# The new skill tree against legacy

Generated by `python tools/deviations.py --write`; do not edit by hand. Legacy Ghost Mode had one tree, the classic one, so 5.x's new tree has no legacy counterpart. Everything on [deviations.md](deviations.md) applies in the new tree too unless its text names the classic tree; this page adds what differs only here.

**The new tree's rules** ([SKL area decisions](skl.md#area-decisions), project owner): every new-tree skill is its legacy counterpart under legacy's name, on 3 levels, with legacy's maximum value at level 3 (a third per level); legacy's tiers scaled ×3/5 (sword 5/12/18, alchemy 5/12/17, signs 4/11/17) gate it in place of 5.x's prerequisites; 5.x's new-only skills and perks are replaced by the legacy skills and perks that had no new-tree slot; general perks are legacy's, 1 point each; passive path bonuses are removed, as in the classic tree. Below: the skills designed afresh, and the deviations beyond these rules.

- New designs: 13
- Deviations: 5; not confirmed by the owner: 0; temporary: 0

## New designs

### [SKL-03 Fast Attack branch](skl.md#skl-03-fast-attack-branch)

- Muscle Memory, new tree `sword_s22` (D1, D53, project owner, 2026-10-06, revised 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-11), tier 1. 5.x's trigger stays (a successful dodge or roll, above), with 6 s for every timer: the first fast attack must hit within 6 s of the dodge, each further one within 6 s of the one before (5.x's 5 s arming window is not used). Levels scale the number of boosted fast attacks, 1/2/3 (D53: 5.x's code boosts one fewer, fixed). Each boosted attack gets +60% attack power and +10% total damage at every level, instead of 5.x's raw-damage multiplier, so fast attacks gain little against armor (armor piercing belongs to strong attacks). Plus legacy's +15% adrenaline gain for fast attacks at level 3 (0.05 per level, unconditional, damage dealt only). Description (level 3): "After a successful dodge or roll, your next 3 Fast Attacks in a row gain 60% attack power and deal 10% more total damage. Adrenaline gain for Fast Attacks is increased by 15%."
- Precise Blows, new tree `sword_s24` (D57, project owner, 2026-10-09), tier 3, replacing 5.x's Three Strikes (on the 4th same-type hit in a row, 20% per level for +40–70% on that hit): the fast-attack mirror of Crushing Blows (SKL-04, D55), keeping legacy's emphasis on crits with a chaining incentive. Fast attacks (melee) get +8% crit chance at every level and +20% crit damage per level (+60% at 3); a fast-attack crit gives fast attacks a further +8% crit chance and +20% crit damage per level for 4 s (shorter than Crushing Blows's 6 s), not stacking, restarted by another fast-attack crit, with the sword effect `fast_attack_buff` while it lasts. At level 3 with the chain up: +16% crit chance, +120% crit damage. Shared effect: Muscle Memory also uses `fast_attack_buff`, so the effect stays on while either skill's bonus is active (as Crushing Blows and Strength Training). Description (level 3): "Increases Fast Attack critical hit power by 60% and critical hit chance by 8%. A Fast Attack critical hit increases Fast Attack critical hit chance by a further 8% and critical hit power by a further 60% for 4 seconds."

### [SKL-04 Strong Attack branch](skl.md#skl-04-strong-attack-branch)

- Crushing Blows, new tree `sword_s25` (legacy's name; 5.x: Crushing Blow) (D55, project owner, 2026-10-08, revised 2026-10-09), tier 3: reworked to keep legacy's emphasis on crits and 5.x's incentive to chain attacks, replacing 5.x's proc. Strong attacks get +8% crit chance at every level and +20% crit damage per level (+60% at 3); a strong-attack crit (Rend included) gives strong attacks a further +8% crit chance and +20% crit damage per level for 6 s (5.x's `duration`), not stacking, restarted by another strong-attack crit, with 5.x's sword effect `strong_attack_buff` while it lasts. At level 3 with the chain up: +16% crit chance, +120% crit damage. Shared effect: 5.x stops a combo effect by its name on the sword (`StopEffect`), and Strength Training uses the same `strong_attack_buff`, so the port keeps the effect on while either skill's bonus is active: one skill's timer or use must not turn it off while the other's is still running. Description (level 3): "Increases Strong Attack critical hit power by 60% and critical hit chance by 8%. A Strong Attack critical hit increases Strong Attack critical hit chance by a further 8% and critical hit power by a further 60% for 6 seconds."
- Strength Training, new tree `sword_s23` (D1, D54, project owner, 2026-10-08), tier 1, replacing 5.x's single bonus: each fast-attack swing that hits adds a stack to the next strong attack (one per swing; 5.x counts once per target), up to 1/2/3 stacks by level, each worth half the one before: +35% attack power and +10% weapon damage, then +17.5%/+5%, then +8.75%/+2.5% (+61.25% attack power and +17.5% weapon damage at 3 stacks). The next strong attack (Rend included) uses up every stack; all stacks expire when none has been added for 6 s (5.x's window, `duration` 6, renewed on each fast hit). Weapon damage is a pool term, attack power goes to the attack-power step. Plus legacy's +15% strong-attack adrenaline gain at level 3 (0.05 per level, unconditional, damage dealt only). Needs new code: 5.x's counter (`ProcessNewSkill_StrengthTraining_Counter`) counts once per target hit and gives one flat bonus. Description (level 3): "Each Fast Attack increases the next Strong Attack's power by 35% and weapon damage by 10%. This stacks up to 3 times, with each stack losing half its effect. Adrenaline gain for Strong Attacks is increased by 15%."

### [SKL-06 Marksmanship branch](skl.md#skl-06-marksmanship-branch)

- Cold Blood, new tree `sword_s34` (D13, D17, project owner, 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-11): it stays a combat skill, at tier 2 (Steady Shot's legacy tier; the new tree has no Steady Shot of its own), and carries both legacy skills. Legacy's Cold Blood adrenaline at every level: each bolt that deals damage gives a sword hit's adrenaline (0.1 × (1 + adrenaline gain, Razor Focus included), Adrenaline Rush applies, split bolts share it; no melee-only bonuses; not for damage over time or dodged, parried or countered bolts). Legacy's Steady Shot damage scaling with level: +16.67 per level (+50 at 3) to every damage type of each bolt hit, a flat addition before the pool (legacy's `xbow_dmg_bonus`; also shown in the character panel's crossbow damage). 5.x's flat 0.3/0.6/1.0 adrenaline per bolt is dropped.

### [SKL-18 Perks](skl.md#skl-18-perks)

- Wolf School `perk_26`, new tree only (D61, project owner, 2026-10-11): the slot is kept and becomes an alchemy-themed medium-armor perk in which bombs help sword attacks, around poison. After an enemy takes bomb damage, melee hits against it with the right oil on the blade get +20% crit chance and +60% crit damage (the oil condition works against poison-immune enemies too); bombs deal +30% weapon damage to poisoned enemies. The window lasts 10 s and is restarted by further bomb damage; the values are totals with four medium armor pieces (+5% crit chance, +15% crit damage and +7.5% bomb damage per piece), like the other school perks. Description: "After an enemy receives bomb damage, blade oils grant 20% critical hit chance and 60% critical hit power against it. Bombs deal 30% more damage against poisoned enemies."

### [STAM-10 Exhaustion: paying from max stamina](stam.md#stam-10-exhaustion-paying-from-max-stamina)

- Exhaustion, new tree only, behind the option "Exhaustion" (on in "Recommended", off in "Vanilla(ish)" and "Turn off"; legacy had no such rule): covered actions without the stamina are paid from max stamina and adrenaline as described above; the options "Max stamina regen" (0.4, 0 to 1), "Adrenaline per stamina point" (0.03, 0 to 0.1) and "Max stamina floor" (40, 10 to 100). The menu cannot hide options per tree, so the four labels say "new skill tree only" (project owner, 2026-11-04).
- the lowered max is stacks of a hidden ability with `<stamina type="add" min="-1"/>` (`SetStatPointMax` is undone by the next recompute); cleared on a character reset or tree switch, at a load with the classic tree in force or the option off, at New Game+ and on meditation; the low-stamina heartbeat (STAM-07), Whirl's adrenaline per tick (STAM-09, also in its description) and the skills restoring a share of max stamina (Attack is the Best Defense, Refreshment) read the full max; Rend's charge and damage and Quen's absorb cost keep the lowered max (project owner, 2026-11-04).
- fractions of a point: the amount owed is kept exactly and the stacks are its whole points rounded up, so a 6.666 attack lowers the max by 7 while 6.666 is owed, and regen pays the exact amount back; the fraction is lost at a load (the stacks are read then). While the option is off or the tree is classic mid-session, existing stacks stay until the next load, meditation or reset, and still regenerate (porter's call, confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-04).
- max stamina regenerates at the combat regen (its attribute and the combat option, halved while guarding, with the armor modifier) in and out of combat, × "Max stamina regen". At the out-of-combat rate (100% of max a second) it came back within seconds of a fight; at the combat rate (measured out of combat: 4.6 points a second at a max of 40, rising with the max; 8 at 100) a full drain to the floor (60 points) takes about 10 s with stamina full (project owner, 2026-11-04).
- which adrenaline counts as "from the paid action": every positive gain while one of Geralt's actions is processed (`ProcessAction`) or his foot attack runs (`DoAttack`), from the payment until the attack's hits are done; at most 3 s (a horseback swing, which waits for its animation) or 2 s (a counter, whose hit comes with its animation), and half a second for Phantom Weapon's delayed heavy strike (a timer the next frame). Gains outside those (signs, potions, other sources) pass (porter's call, confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-04).
- the horse Axii (HORSE-02) is a sign, outside Exhaustion: it needs 50 stamina as before, and with Adrenaline Burst its adrenaline counts against the full max stamina, as without a lowered max (project owner, 2026-11-04).
- HUD and panel details: vanilla's 2 s "stamina needed" stretch is drawn against the full max too, then the hold resumes; the sign icon is un-dimmed when stamina reaches the lowered max (Flash's `setSignIconDimmed`), without the sign-ready flash; the panel's maximum reads "70 (100)" (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-04).

## Deviations

### [SKL-16 Mutation](skl.md#skl-16-mutation)

### [STAM-01 Sign cooldowns](stam.md#stam-01-sign-cooldowns)

- 5.x's Aard finisher (the finisher `man_finisher_aard_01_rp_remaster`, drawn with the new tree's Shock Wave, `magic_s33`) casts a free Aard (its cost disabled); that Aard starts no Aard cooldown and fires no sign-cast decoction hook ([ALCH-10](alch.md#alch-10-decoctions-that-build-up-on-melee-hits-katakan-water-hag-wyvern-alghoul-forktail)), so Aard is ready right after the finisher. Legacy had no such finisher; without the exemption the free Aard put Aard on its 5 s cooldown (whole-port audit A1-2; porter's call, confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-03).

### [XP-09 The new tree's skill-point income (new, owner)](xp.md#xp-09-the-new-trees-skill-point-income-new-owner)

- in the new tree levels 3, 6, 9, …, 48 give no skill point and +150 Vitality, and the other levels a point and no Vitality: 33 points and 2,400 Vitality from levels by level 50 (legacy, one tree: a point and +50 Vitality every level, 49 and 2,450). The new tree's skills cost fewer points (DX1 (a), owner, 2026-10-31)
- past level 50 the new tree keeps the same pace, no skill point on every third level (51, 54, …), and no level past 50 gives Vitality, the third levels included; NG+ the same. Legacy's levels past 50 give a point every level and no Vitality (DX2 (a), owner, 2026-10-31)
