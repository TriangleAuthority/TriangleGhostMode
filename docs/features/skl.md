# SKL: skills, signs and perks

Ghost Mode reworks almost every skill and perk so that each one does what its description says (P7), fits the stamina economy (P2) and the damage formula (P1), and stays close to vanilla's idea of the skill (P8). Source: the changelist's "Skill changes" section (lines 250–467 of [legacy-FullChangelist.txt](../legacy-FullChangelist.txt)), plus the skill fixes in its Bugfixes section.

**How legacy Ghost Mode implements it.** Most changes are new values and new attributes in `geralt_skills.xml` (and its NG+ copy, the expansions' `geralt_skills_ep1/ep2.xml`), read by new or rewritten code in `damageManagerProcessor.ws`, `playerWitcher.ws`, `PlayerAbilityManager.ws`, the sign entity scripts and a few new effects (Melt Armor, Undying). The skill tree layout (`custom/skills/definitions`) moves a few skills between tiers and changes some level counts. Descriptions are new string rows and new tooltip arguments in `characterMenu.ws`.

**What 5.x did in this area.** 5.x has two skill trees ([Two skill trees](../feature-mapping.md#two-skill-trees)): the classic tree (1.32's skills, nearly all cut from 5 levels to 3 with raised values) and the new tree (52 reworked skills and perks, plus 28 skills shared with the classic tree). Several skills Ghost Mode reworked were reworked by 5.x too, differently: Undying, Flood of Anger, Fleet Footed, Deflection, Puppet; some classic skills lost their effect in 5.x's code (Shock Wave without Mutation 6, Pyromaniac). Facts that hold for the whole area, from the code (not tested in game):

- **Shared skills ignore tier points**, in both trees: `W3PlayerAbilityManager.CanLearnSkill` checks `requiredPointsSpent` only for classic-only skills; shared skills are gated by their `required_skills`. A tier change of a shared skill (legacy's "Rend is now tier 2") cannot be made through its points.
- **Passive path bonuses go to every equipped skill**, classic ones included (`OnSkillEquip`, `OnSkillUnequip`, `OnSkillEquippedLevelChange`); 5.x's function that would limit them to reworked and shared skills (`AddSkillPassiveBonusesForEquipped`) has no caller. 5.x also adds `survival_vitality` (+1% max Vitality per level of an equipped perk) and makes the potion-duration passive raise bomb damage.
- **Shared skills use new string keys in both trees** (e.g. `skill_rend_description`); legacy's overrides of the old keys (`skill_desc_sword_s2`) would not show for them. Classic-only skills keep the old keys. The new tree has its own character menu (`characterMenuDupe.ws`) with its own tooltip functions.
- **5.x's new tree has no intensity skills** (Aard, Igni, Yrden, Quen, Axii Intensity), no Pyromaniac, no Quen Discharge and no Killing Spree; the port gives each a new-tree home (D58, D60).

Unit lists: [attribution.csv](attribution.csv): 1,416 units attributed to SKL-01 to SKL-19 (mapped from the changelist and sweeps of every skill, perk, sign and alchemy keyword); 973 of them are SKL-19's localization rows (108 strings × 9 languages). Legacy changes inside SKL's units that belong to other areas (the damage pool, toxicity, armor sets, glyphwords, mutations, the character stats popup's skill lines, horse Axii) are attributed to and ported by those areas. Port status: SKL-01 to SKL-19 ported and verified in game in both trees; **signed off** by the project owner on 2026-10-17.

## Area decisions

The per-skill pass is complete (project owner, 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-11): every skill and perk of both trees is decided (D43 decided 2026-10-16; D45 decided as one SKL-19 pass, the porter's call, confirmed by the owner 2026-10-16) ([open decisions](#open-decisions)). The damage formula is in place ([dmg.md](dmg.md)): where a skill's bonus sits (raw damage and its additive pool, attack power, final damage) decides what its numbers are worth, DMG's functions host the skill terms SKL ports ([DMG's follow-ups from SKL](dmg.md#follow-ups-from-skl)), and the test dummy and damage probe ([development.md](../development.md#testing-in-game)) measure them. Decisions are numbered (D1, D2, …) in the entries and kept in [skl-decisions.md](../skill-trees/skl-decisions.md); the rules are in [balance.md](balance.md).

**Structure**

- **The trees are separated** (project owner, 2026-10-05; [plan](balance.md#two-skill-trees-the-plan)): the 28 skills 5.x shares between the trees stop being shared, and each tree has its own values, levels, tiers and descriptions. Trees are switched only through the character reset (D44, [SKL-01](#skl-01-skill-system-rules)).
- **The classic tree is legacy Ghost Mode's tree, and the default** (D46, project owner, 2026-10-05): legacy's skills, values, maximum levels (mostly 5) and tier points (sword 8/20/30, alchemy 8/20/28, signs 6/18/28).
- **The new tree carries legacy's skills on 3 levels** (project owner, 2026-10-09): every new-tree skill is its legacy counterpart, giving legacy's max-level total at level 3 (a third per level), except the [new designs](#new-designs) below. 5.x's new-only skills and perks are replaced by the legacy skills and perks that had no new-tree slot (D58, D60, D61).
- **The new tree drops 5.x's prerequisite tree and uses legacy's tiers** (D59, project owner, 2026-10-09): 5.x's tree gates nothing in practice (any skill is a few points from a root), so a new-tree skill is unlocked by points spent in its path, at the tier of its legacy counterpart, as in the classic tree; 5.x's `required_skills` chains, their connection lines and the grid positions are not used. The tier points are legacy's scaled ×3/5 to the 3 levels (project owner, 2026-10-11): sword 5/12/18, alchemy 5/12/17, signs 4/11/17. The general skills have no gate and no prerequisites, as legacy's perks. Ported and verified ([SKL-02](#skl-02-skill-tree-option), slice 3, 2026-10-16). The levels and tier points stay as decided; their pace is checked with XP's skill-point income, not before (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16; [open decisions](#open-decisions)). The tree may return later.
- **New-tree skills take legacy's names** wherever a legacy counterpart exists (project owner, 2026-10-11): Deflection (5.x: Arrow Deflection), Fleet Footed, Shock Wave, Puppet (5.x: Puppetmaster), Rage Management (5.x: Anger Management), Metabolism Boosts (5.x: Metabolic Boost), Connoisseur, Restoration, Crippling Strikes, Crippling Shot, Precise Blows and so on. The entries use these names; 5.x's name is given where it differs.
- **General perks are legacy's in both trees** (D49, project owner, 2026-10-09, made explicit 2026-10-11; [SKL-18](#skl-18-perks)): every general perk with a legacy counterpart is legacy's perk in both trees: 1 point, 1 level, legacy's effect and full value; the only general perks without a legacy counterpart are the redesigned Wolf School and Viper School (D61), also 1 point and 1 level.
- **Passive path bonuses are removed in both trees** (project owner, 2026-10-04; [SKL-01](#skl-01-skill-system-rules)).

- Muscle Memory `sword_s22` (D1, D53): attack power for fast attacks, more after a successful dodge or roll ([SKL-03](#skl-03-fast-attack-branch)).

- Strength Training `sword_s23` (D1, D54): attack power for strong attacks and for the strike after one ([SKL-04](#skl-04-strong-attack-branch)).
- Crushing Blows `sword_s25` (D55): crit power, stacked by strong attacks until the next crit ([SKL-04](#skl-04-strong-attack-branch)).
- Cold Blood `sword_s34` (D13, D17): a combat skill at tier 2 that also carries Steady Shot's bolt damage ([SKL-06](#skl-06-marksmanship-branch)).
- Wolf School `perk_26` and Viper School `perk_28` (D61): with medium armor, crits against incapacitated enemies (Wolf) and against poisoned or bleeding ones (Viper) ([SKL-18](#skl-18-perks)).
- The new tree's 3 levels with scaled tier points (D59, above).
- The skill-point income rule (project owner, 2026-10-11), for the XP area: in the new tree, no skill point on every third level, with the Vitality bonus concentrated into those levels ([feature-mapping.md](../feature-mapping.md#progression)).

**Per branch** (details in the entries)

- **Combat** ([SKL-03](#skl-03-fast-attack-branch) to [SKL-07](#skl-07-battle-trance-branch)): legacy in both trees for Whirl, Crippling Strikes (D2), Rend, Sunder Armor (D56), Deflection (D5, D6), Fleet Footed, Counterattack (D9), Deadly Precision (D10, D11), Lightning Reflexes, Anatomical Knowledge (D14), Crippling Shot, Resolve, Undying (D15), Razor Focus and Flood of Anger (D16); no instant-kill cooldown for the player (D12). The classic Muscle Memory and Strength Training give +50% attack power at level 5, counted once (D47, a deviation in implementation). The new tree's Muscle Memory, Precise Blows, Strength Training, Crushing Blows and Cold Blood are new designs.
- **Signs** ([SKL-08](#skl-08-aard) to [SKL-12](#skl-12-axii)): legacy in both trees for every sign and sign skill (B7; D18, D20 to D26). The new sign grid's seven 5.x-only slots carry legacy's sign skills that had no new-tree home (D58, project owner, 2026-10-09): `magic_s35` Igni Intensity (5.x: Catalyst), `magic_s36` Axii Intensity (Fortify Signs), `magic_s37` Yrden Intensity (Chain Reaction), `magic_s38` Quen Intensity (freed by Focus, D40), `magic_s39` Aard Intensity (Sidestep), `magic_s40` Quen Discharge (Aftershock), `magic_s41` Pyromaniac (Resonance), each at its legacy tier with legacy's max-level values at level 3; the 5.x skills are dropped. Melt Armor does not take Pyromaniac's burn chance, nor 5.x's own +30%.
- **Alchemy** ([SKL-13](#skl-13-brewing) to [SKL-17](#skl-17-trial-of-the-grasses)): the alchemy tree is legacy's in both trees (D60, project owner, 2026-10-09; D27 to D34). Refreshment takes `alchemy_s24` (5.x: High Tolerance), Fixative `alchemy_s25` (Volatile Compound), Steady Aim `alchemy_s23` (Toxic Shock), Killing Spree `alchemy_s22` (Debilitating Poison) and Synergy `alchemy_s26` (Potent Sting; 5.x's Synergy perk `perk_43` is freed); the 5.x skills are dropped.
- **Perks** ([SKL-18](#skl-18-perks)): legacy's perks in both trees (D37, D39, D41), with In Combat's Fires' defect fixed (D38, a deviation). In the new tree Focus is a general perk again on `perk_37` (D40), and Trick Shot, Heavy Artillery and In Combat's Fires take `perk_27`, `perk_43` and `perk_44` (D61, revised 2026-11-07); Gorged on Power is not in the new tree; Manticore School, Element of Surprise, Elemental Attunement and 5.x's Synergy perk are dropped; Wolf School `perk_26` and Viper School `perk_28` are new designs. A slot that carries another skill than 5.x's shows that skill's classic icon (project owner, 2026-11-07).

**Damage and descriptions**

- **Raw-damage bonuses are additive pool terms** ([DD1](dmg.md#area-decisions), project owner, 2026-10-06; settles D4): every percentage bonus to raw damage joins legacy's additive pool. Exceptions are deliberate ([DMG-04](dmg.md#dmg-04-additive-raw-damage-pool)): Crippling Strikes' bonus is a final-damage multiplier (D2), and the new Muscle Memory and Strength Training give attack power instead of a pool term (D1, D54). The 5.x new-tree multipliers DMG hosts today go when SKL replaces their skills ([DMG's follow-ups](dmg.md#follow-ups-from-skl)).
- **Description wording** (project owner, 2026-10-08), for every skill and perk description: a bonus that adds to the raw-damage pool says "weapon damage", one that adds attack power says "attack power", one that multiplies final damage says "total damage" ([SKL-19](#skl-19-skill-and-perk-descriptions)). Exception: "power" alone where "attack power" would put "attack" twice in a row (Strength Training's "the next Strong Attack's power"). Two more rules (project owner, 2026-10-16; SKL audit F35, F36): a bonus to critical hit damage says "critical hit power", legacy's term, in both trees and all nine languages (each language's "power" word); a bomb's damage bonus says plain "damage" ("weapon damage" reads wrong for a bomb), whatever step it sits in (Heavy Artillery, Wolf School).
- **Description numbers** (project owner, 2026-10-09): adrenaline amounts rounded to two decimals, percentages to whole points (Razor Focus's levels read 0.42/0.83/1.25 and 8%/17%/25%).

**To revisit when balancing** (project owner, 2026-10-09): what a crossbow build looks like (the owner finds Steady Shot dull; its damage is legacy's for now); a poison build; more synergy between bombs and other builds. 5.x's dropped skills (Toxic Shock, Debilitating Poison, Potent Sting, Volatile Compound, Element of Surprise's bomb-to-melee bonus) are the starting material.

**Small differences accepted at the SKL audit** (2026-10-16; no case found in play, so not recorded as deviations): Counterattack's crit asks for a light or heavy attack that is not a bolt, where legacy asks `IsActionMelee()`; Strong Attack's armor piercing keeps a melee check legacy dropped; the new tree's Crippling Shot needs a bolt, the classic one any ranged crit; Crippling Strikes also rolls its bleeding on a hit that starts a finisher or dismemberment (legacy skips those); with Mutagen 8 legacy turns a knockdown into a stagger before its Quen rules, the port after, so the alternate Quen breaks; `OnSignCastPerformed` runs at the start of Firestream and the active Quen (5.x), where legacy ran it at the end; no Quen hit effect against a web projectile; a few rounded description numbers do not add up (the new tree's Whirl at level 2: 3% per attacker, up to 17%; Hunter Instinct 7% and 20%, 13% and 40%); under the new profile the classic-only skills are hidden but `CanLearnSkill` does not refuse them (the menu cannot reach them). Test-only state outside `tests/` (`gmLastRendAoEHits` in `skills/rend.ws`) is accepted.

**What it does:** "Passive skills bonuses (adrenaline gain, stamina regen, potion duration) were removed from the game." Not in the changelist: a skill ability tagged `GlobalPassiveBonus` (perk_4, 10, 12, 22, Sun and Stars' parts, Prolonged Effects) follows the level of an equipped skill (vanilla added it only on equip, so a level bought while equipped was lost). Bugfixes 83–84 and 179: skills stuck in locked slots can be removed, a skill cannot be swapped into a slot of the wrong colour, a swap re-applies the bonuses. A respec sets toxicity to 0.

**Implementation (legacy):** the three passive abilities' attributes commented out in `geralt_skills.xml`; their add and remove blocks commented out in `W3PlayerAbilityManager.OnSkillEquip`, `OnSkillUnequip`, `OnSkillEquippedLevelChange` and `ResetCharacterDev`; `W3PlayerWitcher.OnSpawned` removes them on every load. Slot fixes: `UnequipSkill` (`GetSkillSlotIndex(slotID, false)`), `CR4CharacterMenu.CheckAdditionalSlots` (new) and `OnSwapSkill`. `Debug_ClearCharacterDevelopment` rebuilt as a respec that keeps level, XP and points.

**5.x:** keeps and extends the passives (above); none of the slot fixes; the level sync only for perk_30, 33 and 35 (`AdjustAbilitiesOnLevelChange`). A skill in a locked slot still counts as equipped (`IsSkillEquipped` reads every slot), but `UnequipSkill` and `GetSkillSlotID` skip locked slots, so it cannot be removed; `UpdateMutationSkillSlots` locks the mutation slots before it unequips their skills, so unequipping the mutation outside the menu leaves its skills stuck. `OnSwapSkill` equips the first skill while it still sits in the other slot, and the unequip from that slot then takes back what the equip added (verified 2026-10-16: after 5.x's sequence Sun and Stars had no day or night part left).

**Decisions:** remove the passives in both trees, including 5.x's `survival_vitality` and the potion-duration bomb damage, by zeroing the abilities (covers old saves and both trees), plus the load cleanup (project owner, 2026-10-04). The passive lines are gone from the tooltips (SKL-19). The level sync ports generically, merged with 5.x's perk_30/33/35 case so nothing is added twice. D43 (project owner, 2026-10-16): the slot fixes are SKL-01's, not FIX's. In Ghost Mode's profiles every skill with a `GlobalPassiveBonus` ability has one level (in the classic profile perk_1, 4, 10, 12 and 22; in the new perk_30, 33, 35, 38 and 42; D49), and Prolonged Effects reads its level from the skill (SKL-13), so the level sync has work only for a character still on 5.x's definitions (3-level new-tree perks until `gmreset()`) and for any later multi-level skill. D44 decided (2026-10-05): the respec becomes a safe character reset in the mod, extended to 5.x's point bookkeeping and the tree profile ([balance.md](balance.md#two-skill-trees-the-plan)).

- **Deviation:** the character reset is built on 5.x's own respec (`ResetCharacterDev` and `ResetMutationsDev`, what the Potion of Clearance and the Restoring Potion run) plus legacy's extras (greater mutagens spent on research returned, skill mutagens unequipped, toxicity 0); legacy deletes and rebuilds the level, ability and effect managers. The result for the player is the same, on the path the game itself uses (project owner, 2026-10-11).
- **Deviation:** the character reset leaves gear and sword oils alone; legacy unequips every item, the horse's included, and removes the oils from both swords. A skill reset does not need it (project owner, 2026-10-11).
- **Deviation:** the character reset is refused in combat (5.x cannot unequip a mutation there); legacy's runs at any time (project owner, 2026-10-12).
- **Deviation:** every respec through `ResetCharacterDev` (the Potion of Clearance, `gmreset()`, 5.x's other callers) first empties every skill slot by its ID, locked slots and the fourth mutation slot included; legacy's Potion of Clearance left a skill in a locked slot socketed at level 0, so its bonuses stayed when it was later removed (legacy's own respec rebuilt the managers and had no such case) (audit F28; confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16).
- **Deviation:** the character menu unequips the skills in locked slots each time it opens, before it builds its data; legacy runs the check from the menu's `setMutationBonusMode` (on opening with the mutation system on, and on equipping a mutation). The same result for the player, and it also covers a character without the mutation system (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16).

**Skill slots unlock by level, as legacy** (project owner, 2026-10-12): the pace of slot unlocks is an important control on player power. 1.32 and legacy open a slot when Geralt's level reaches its `unlockedOnLevel` (0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 22, 26, 30). 5.x compares the same numbers with the total of skill points (`UpdateSkillSlots`: free + used, with mutation research counted twice), so Places of Power and quest items open slots early and any change to the skill point income would change the pace; after a load 5.x also leaves every slot but the first locked until a skill point is gained or the new character menu opens. The port restores the level rule in both trees, which also ends the stale state after a load. **The skill mutagen slots** (`unlockedAtLevel` 2, 9, 16, 28) **follow the level too** (porter's call, confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-31: legacy's rule, extending the owner's rule for skill slots): 5.x's `IsSkillMutagenSlotUnlocked` and its new character menu's `UpdateMutagens` compare them with the same skill-point total, so in 5.x they open a level late without Places of Power (3, 10, 17, 29) and earlier with them, and XP-09's smaller income would move them further in the new tree (the fourth at about level 41 without Places of Power); both functions are replaced with the level rule. **The locked-slot tooltips say the level** (XP audit F2, `skills/skillSlots.ws`): the new character menu, which both trees use, filled 5.x's "Unlocked at N Ability Points acquired." with the slot's unlock level; wrappers of `CR4CharacterDupeMenu.OnGetLockedTooltipData` and `OnGetMutagenLockedTooltipData` set 1.32's and legacy's text, "Slot unlocked at level: N" (`panel_character_tooltip_skill_locked_desc` and `panel_character_tooltip_mutagen_locked_desc`, 1070951 and 1070952, in every language of the game; no new strings; not a deviation).

**Slices:**

1. **Character reset** (D44; `skills/characterReset.ws`). `gmreset()` refunds every skill point (those not from levels included), clears mutation research and returns its skill points and greater mutagens, unequips the mutation, skills and skill mutagens, sets toxicity to 0, and keeps level, experience, money and gear. The level-based points are then set to what the tree in force gives for the level (`GMGetLevelSkillPoints`: classic 1 per level, the new tree none on every level divisible by 3, XP-09), and Geralt's level abilities are swapped to the tree's (`Lvl<n>` or `GMLvlNew<n>`, the Vitality percentage kept); the points the levels gave before are counted by the level ability held for each level, which records the rule it was gained under. Verified 2026-10-11 by scripted calls (`gmtest_skl01_setup`, `gmreset()`, `gmtest_skl01_check`) with both tree settings: everything refunded and unequipped, unchanged after a save and reload, refused in combat (`gmtest_skl01_combat`); the per-tree level points verified by script with XP slice X5 ([xp.md](xp.md#xp-09-the-new-trees-skill-point-income-new-owner)).
2. **Skill slots by level** (`skills/skillSlots.ws`: `@replaceMethod` of `UpdateSkillSlots`, wrappers of `PostInit` and `RemasterSkillSlotReset`). Verified 2026-10-12 (`gmtest_skl01_slots`, scripted): the slots follow the level, not the skill points, also after a reset and a reload. The mutagen slots by level verified by script in both trees (XP slice X5, `gmtest_xp09_levels`), the tooltips by script (`gmtest_skl01_tooltip`) and on screen (2026-11-01). Not tested: the conversion of a save from before 5.0.
3. **No passive bonuses** (`skills/passiveBonuses.ws`, `gm_skills.xml`). The passive abilities' values are 0 in both trees: `sword_adrenalinegain`, `magic_staminaregen`, `alchemy_potionduration` (which 5.x also reads for bomb and damage-over-time damage) and `survival_vitality`; the copies 5.x still adds on equip do nothing, and a wrapper of `W3PlayerWitcher.OnSpawned` removes them on every load (legacy's cleanup). Verified 2026-10-15 (`gmtest_skl01_passives`, scripted reads): all four read 0, none on Geralt after a load, equipped copies change nothing. The cleanup of a save that carries copies: compiles only.
4. **Level sync** (`skills/passiveBonuses.ws`): a wrapper of `OnSkillEquippedLevelChange` sets the count of a `GlobalPassiveBonus` ability to the equipped skill's level after 5.x's own handling, so perk_30, 33 and 35 are not added twice. Verified 2026-10-16 (`gmtest_skl01_sync`, scripted) on classic and new-tree perks.
5. **Slot fixes** (D43; `skills/skillSlots.ws`). `UnequipSkill` removes a skill from a locked slot too (legacy's `GetSkillSlotIndex(slotID, false)`); the character menu unequips the skills in locked slots when it opens (legacy's `CheckAdditionalSlots`); its `OnSwapSkill` refuses a skill whose colour is not the equipped mutation's for a mutation slot, and empties both slots before equipping. Verified 2026-10-16 in both trees (`gmtest_skl01_slotfix`, the menu opened for real with `dupemenu()`, setup and swaps scripted), again 2026-11-14 with `OnSwapSkill` as a wrapper; `GMTest_skl01_rules` in `gmtest_regress()`.
6. **Reset fixes** (audit F28, F29; `skills/characterReset.ws`). A wrapper of `W3PlayerAbilityManager.ResetCharacterDev` empties every skill slot by its ID first (5.x unequipped through `GetSkillSlotID`, which skips locked slots, and `ResetMutationsDev` passes slot indexes where `UnequipSkill` takes IDs, so a skill in the fourth mutation slot stayed socketed at level 0 with its bonuses); a wrapper of `W3PlayerWitcher.ResetCharacterDev` sets toxicity to 0 (legacy), so the Potion of Clearance does too. Verified 2026-10-16 in both trees (`gmtest_sklh_reset`, scripted, through `gmreset()` and a Potion of Clearance).

**What it does:** "Almost all the descriptions for skills and perks in-game were changed for clarity and to reflect mod changes" (changelist 256). Legacy: new tooltip arguments in `CR4CharacterMenu.Get{Sword,Sign,Alchemy}SkillsTooltipDescription` and `GetPerkTooltipDescription`, the passive lines removed; 108 skill and perk strings in 9 languages; skill lines in the character stats popup (UI).

**5.x:** two menus with their own tooltip functions; shared skills on new string keys (above); the new menu appends a passive path line to every tooltip, and has no arguments for some classic skills (`$I$`, `$S$`, `$F$` shown raw: Gourmet, Advanced Pyrotechnics and Battle Frenzy in the classic tree, 2026-10-16).

**How it works:** one wrapper of the new menu's tooltip function, which serves both trees (SKL-02). Each skill Ghost Mode describes has one text (one per level where the text changes with the level), shared by the two trees; its numbers are read from the skill's ability in force (`GM_C_<skill>` or the new tree's values, through `GetSkillAttributeValue`) for the level shown, so each tree shows its own per-level values and the same totals at its maximum level. A skill without a Ghost Mode text keeps 5.x's, without the passive path lines (SKL-01). Ghost Mode's own string keys (`gm_skill_desc_*`, `gm_skill_name_*`); where the decided text is 5.x's own string, 5.x's key (Resolve, Lightning Reflexes). Names: a new-tree skill takes its legacy counterpart's name through the new tree's profile block, a classic skill legacy's name through the classic block; 5.x copies the new and shared skills' string keys from the XML at each load, so the profile in force is laid on again after it (both trees).

**Decisions:** D45 (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16): descriptions are one SKL-19 pass, done after every mechanic was ported, in slices by branch, each covering both trees. Names (project owner, 2026-10-11): a new-tree skill takes its legacy counterpart's name. Wording (project owner, 2026-10-08): a bonus to the raw-damage pool is "weapon damage", an attack-power bonus is "attack power", a final-damage multiplier is "total damage" ([DMG's terms](dmg.md)); "power" alone where "attack" would be written twice in a row ("the next Strong Attack's power", Strength Training). A crit damage bonus is "critical hit power" and a bomb's damage bonus plain "damage" (project owner, 2026-10-16; audit F35, F36). Numbers (project owner, 2026-10-09): adrenaline amounts rounded to two decimals, percentages to whole points (e.g. 0.42/0.83/1.25, 8%/17%/25%).

- Which text (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16): the owner's decided text where there is one (the entries above), in both trees where the skill is the same in both; otherwise legacy's text where it is accurate for the ported mechanics and follows the wording rule; otherwise a new text. Where a skill's text changes with its level (Deflection, Counterattack; the new tree's Crushing Blows at level 1, "Stacks once.", `gm_skill_desc_crushing_blows_1`), each level has its own text.
- A percentage below one point is shown with up to two decimals (Rend's 0.2% per stamina point at level 1 of the classic tree), so it does not read 0 (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16).
- Translations (porter's call, not confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16): legacy's translation where legacy's text is kept, or where the decided text says what legacy's said (Undying, Flood of Anger, Razor Focus); new texts translated by the porter into the eight other languages, to be reviewed by native speakers; a language whose legacy row is English, empty or has other arguments gets the English text. The new language tables (br, de, es, fr, it, pl) carry every existing Ghost Mode row (legacy's translation where legacy has the id, else English), so adding a table blanks no string.
- Far-Reaching Aard shows 5.x's own classic text, "Increases Aard's range by 3 yard(s).", for the decided "by 3 yards" (the same text, already in every language) (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-16).

### Open decisions

SKL's decisions (D1–D61: questions, what is decided) are kept in [skl-decisions.md](../skill-trees/skl-decisions.md), next to the skills' texts in both games, with one row per skill of every tree; the entries above refer to them by number. Why: the ×3/5 tiers match the 3/5 levels, so a capstone takes 3/5 of legacy's points in both trees' terms; with the new tree's income rule ([XP-09](xp.md#xp-09-the-new-trees-skill-point-income-new-owner): no skill point on every third level, about 2/3 of legacy's income) a path's capstone arrives at about 0.9 of legacy's level, a single-path build's pace close to legacy's.
