Known bugs
==========

Reloading from new checkpoints during lifting a curse from botchling phase of Baron's quest
will cause the botchling to disappear. The bug is strictly visual and Axiing him still works
(technically you Axii the baron and not him anyway). The botchling will reappear once you
reach the destination.

New mastercrafted kits for leveling up weapons and armors can in fact be applied to items at
100% durability: press E (use) and select and item to do so. Drag and drop only works if the
durability is less than 100%.

Igni Firestream appears to be damaging out of range enemies that are positioned behind the
current target - the bug is strictly visual, you see the floating damage numbers, but their
health is not in fact decreased (vanilla bug).

Price modifiers for certain Blood and Wine quest are not applied (vanilla bug).

Certain merchants in Blood and Wine don't have price modifiers (vanilla bug).

If you encounter a rare vanilla bug with HP regen being blocked after a fistfight minigame,
save and reload to fix the problem.

The mod should fix the problem with oils being stuck on a blade forever, but if you have it
on your older save (vanilla or older mod version), just drop the affected sword and pick it
up to have its stats fixed.

Damage formula
==============

(unnecessary details aside)
Vanilla:
damage = (weapon_damage * attack_power - armor) * (1 - damage_resistance)
Modded:
damage = (weapon_damage - armor) * attack_power * (1 - damage_resistance)

The place at which Armor is subtracted makes the biggest difference: now you need to actually
penetrate enemy's Armor with your weapon to deal any damage. With Attack Power increasing
weapon damage directly in vanilla, Armor very quickly becomes irrelevant along with Strong
Attacks, Armor Piercing stats and runes, etc. With the new formula in the mod, Fast Attacks
can do nothing if they can't get through Armor so you either need to go for Armor Piercing
or switch to Strong Attacks/use other means to negate/ignore Armor.

The descriptions for skills and buffs all follow the same set of rules:
- If something increases your weapon damage, it increases your base damage value and helps
  with penetrating enemy's Armor;
- If something increases your Attack Power, it modifies the damage that got through Armor;
- If something increases you Armor Penetration, it doesn't directly increase you damage,
  but rather decrease enemy's Armor value;
- If something decreases enemy's damage resistance, it prevents an enemy from reducing the
  damage you can potentially deal to them;
- If something decreases or increases the damage received (or the final damage dealt), then
  it modifies the final damage value after all the Armor, Attack Power bonuses and resistances
  were applied.

Stamina changes
===============

Since v3.0 the mod introduces stamina based combat. However, if you don't like this type of
combat and think that TW3 is better with vanilla stamina, you can switch the feature off in
mod's options.

There is now an audio cue to indicate low stamina - effect strength and low stamina threshold
are configurable in options. Makes it much easier to track your stamina in the heat of the
combat. (configurable, can be switched off)

Each action in combat has stamina cost attached to it (configurable) - if you don't have
enough stamina, you won't be able to perform that action. Exception - parrying. You can parry
with low stamina, but your parry will be reduced to partial.

Base stamina cost for signs was reduced to 40 points (configurable in options). Cooldown was
introduced for signs (5 sec for normal and 7.5 sec for alternative cast, configurable, can be
turned off completely).

Medium armor is stamina-neutral, light armor (and no armor) reduces stamina costs for melee
actions (signs are unchanged), heavy armor increases stamina costs for all actions, including
signs.

There are items, skills and potions that increase your stamina regen/decrease stamina costs.

Default stamina regen rate is bigger than vanilla - 15 points/sec instead of 10 points/sec
(configurable). Stamina regen when guarded is now 50% of the normal value (was a fixed value
unaffected by buffs in vanilla).

Armor stamina regen bonuses and penalties are removed (can still be turned on in options).

All actions pause stamina regen for 1 sec (configurable). Getting damaged pauses stamina regen
for 1 sec or less - depending on damage severity.

The same logic is also applied to NPCs.

General player abilities
========================

- Passive attack and sign power bonuses were removed from Geralt's levelups: each level now
  adds 50 points to base Geralt's health - and nothing more.
- Default health regen for Geralt in and out of combat is now zero.
- Headshots and backstabs add 25% to Attack Power (was zero).
- Now you only gain adrenaline for actually dealing the damage.
- Adrenaline loss on damage scaling changed: now you lose 0.01 point of Adrenaline per
  1% of health lost, resulting in 1 point lost on 100% health lost.
- Player health is no longer forced to 2000 during fistfight mini-games - actual health is

Horse combat

- Attacking from horse no longer drains 100% stamina.
- Attacks from horseback drain lightAttackCost*3 stamina.
- All the attacks from horseback are considered heavy (same as vanilla).
- Bonus damage when attacking from horseback now depends on weapon damage.
- Higher speed means higher bonus damage: up to x2 when attacking at full speed.
- Attacking at maximum speed adds knockdown to the attack.

Dodging, parrying and countering
================================

In-game description:
Wolf School defensive techniques: parry, counter, dodge. Successful parry, counter and dodge
reduce damage taken to zero and prevent all negative effects. Successful countering grants
additional 25% chance to critical hit. Partial parry reduces damage taken by 50% and prevents
bleeding. Rolling grants immunity to all knockdown and stagger effects and allows to dodge
arrows. Some of the attacks cannot be dodged. Arrows can be parried.

Dodging/rolling costs stamina and is NOT intended for moving around the battleground! Move
normally or use sprinting, but do not spam dodge button to just get closer to an enemy or get
away from it.

Geralt can now initiate sprinting with any percent of stamina left. You can still sprint for
3 seconds for free if you have 100% stamina, same as vanilla.

Dodging works similar to vanilla:
- if an attack can be dodged and you're not dodging toward the attacker, you will avoid the
  damage completely;
- if an attack cannot be dodged and you're inside its hitbox - you will get hit no matter
  what;
- dodging safe angle is down to 120 degrees (was 150 in vanilla), you can increase it with
  light armor bonus (+30 deg to safe angle);
- dodging angle is the angle distance between attack direction and dodge direction, it goes
  from zero (when dodging directly away from the attack) to 180 deg (when dodging directly
  toward the attacker), so the bigger the number is - the better;
- Fleet Footed reduces the damage taken while dodging (aka graze damage) by 10% per skill
  level; it doesn't matter if the attack can or cannot be dodged - if you are dodging or
  rolling, any damage you take is reduced.

Parrying:
- parrying at attack reduces the damage to zero and prevents all the negative effects;
- light attacks can always be parried, same as vanilla;
- heavy attacks that don't have knockdown effect (golem's AoE, etc) can be parried partially:
  the damage is reduced by 50%, bleeding effect is prevented - this is called a partial parry;
- you can parry (but not counter) with not enough stamina: this will reduce the parry to
  partial parry and drain your remaining stamina;
- parrying a heavy attack drains more stamina than parrying a light one.

Countering:
- all the light attacks can be countered, same as vanilla;
- partial counters work the same as partial parries;
- counter is now considered a light attack, so all the light attack bonuses apply;
- unlike vanilla, countering grants you bonuses and not penalties - your damage on counters
  is not reduced, it stays the same as your normal light attack, and you get +25% to critical
  hit chance;
- Counterattack skill improves your counters by increasing attack power and crit chance and
  by adding knockdown.

Armor
=====

Each armor piece has two basic properties: Armor value (see the damage formula above) and
resistances. Armor value is defined by item level: higher level means more Armor. Resistances
are defined by armor class.

There are now three distinct classes of armor in the game:
- Light armor: offers high mobility (-30% to melee actions stamina cost when wearing all 4
  pieces), but close to zero protection (very low resistances).
- Medium armor: no stamina penalties or bonuses, offers medium protection (medium
  resistances).
- Heavy armor: low mobility (+50% to melee actions stamina cost when wearing all 4 pieces),
  casting a sign also costs 50% more (+12.5% penalty per piece), very good protection (high
  resistances).

By default there are no stamina regen bonuses/penalties for light/heavy armor in the mod, but
there's an option to turn them on.

The mod introduces set bonuses for wearing a full set (4 items) of generic (non-witcher)
light/medium/heavy armor:
- Light set bonus: +30 deg to safe angle distance while dodging.
- Medium set bonus: +0.25 points/sec to toxicity regen rate.
- Heavy set bonus: +10% to all damage resistances.

Witcher sets do not grant generic bonuses, but have their specific bonuses instead. All tiers
of witcher sets have access to the minor set bonus and grandmaster sets also grant full set
bonus for having all 6 pieces equipped.

Minor witcher set bonuses (activated when you have at least 3 pieces equipped):
- Kaer Morhen set: new set introduced by the mod, it's an upgrade to starting items available
  from the beginning of the game. The set starts at level 2 and can be upgraded to levels 9,
  19 and 27. Set bonus: Stamina regeneration rate is increased by 1 for each additional
  enemy in combat (up to +5 max). The set contains 4 pieces of armor and no swords.
- Griffin set: Casting a sign reduces stamina cost of all the subsequent actions taken by 5%
  for each piece of the set (-30% for all 6 pieces). Lasts for 2.5 sec.
- Feline (Cat) set: Chained fast hits add +2.5% to Attack Power for each piece of the set
  (+15% for all 6 pieces), up to +60% max. Finishing a chain of fast hits with a strong hit
  discharges the sword doubling the current Attack Power bonus. The bonus resets when the
  chain of hits stops and at the end of the combat.
- Ursine (Bear) set: taking damage increases your adrenaline (0.035 per piece, 0.21 max), you
  get additional bonus to attack power for adrenaline on top of the ones you might have from
  skills (+3.5% per piece, +21% max per adrenaline point).
- Wolven set: Each piece increases the effectiveness of an oil applied to a sword by 16.7%
  (up to 100%). So with a tier 3 oil that has +25% to Attack Power, +5% to critical hit chance
  and +25% to critical hit power, having all 6 pieces of the set equipped will up those
  numbers to +50%, +10% and +50% respectively, doubling the bonuses you get from applying
  oils.
- Viper set: Grants poison immunity. Poisons heal by 10% of poison damage for each set piece
  equipped (up to 60% for all 6 pieces).
- Manticore set: Bombs are thrown without any delay and can deal critical damage. Each set
  piece increases bomb critical hit chance by 5% (30% for all 6 pieces) and bomb critical hit
  power by 10% (60% for all 6 pieces).

Full witcher set bonuses (activated when you have all 6 items equipped, only available for
grandmaster upgrades):

- Feline (Cat) set: Critical hits with a sword double Adrenaline gained.
- Ursine (Bear) set: Damage dealt with a strong attack restores 33.4% stamina spent on that
  attack.

  to a sword increases critical hit chance by 5% and critical hit power by 10%.
- Manticore set: The maximum number of charges for each alchemy item is increased by 1.

Special non-witcher sets:
- Tesham Mutna set: Dealing damage in combat heals 0.5% of maximum Vitality for each piece of
  the set (3% max).

complete her personal quest), enchanter (HoS) and grandmaster (B&W).

Swords
======

Steel swords/secondary weapons use slashing damage and silver swords use silver damage.
Slashing/silver damage is defined by item level - the bigger the level is, the bigger the
damage gets. Weapons can have additional damages like piercing, bludgeoning, fire or poison,
but their main damage is always slashing for steel weapons and silver for silver weapons no
matter what.

All types of damage are processed separately. This means that if your sword has slashing and
fire damages, both of those damages are reduced by victim's armor and resistances (resistances
are damage type dependent) and increased by your bonuses.

Damage over time (DoT) is not reduced by armor, but it can be reduced by victim's resistances
to this specific DoT.

Random weapons get additional bonuses depending on their quality and level: bigger quality
means more bonuses, bigger level means bigger values on bonuses.

You can level up your weapons with mastercrafted kits. These are available at Hattori (after
you complete his personal quest), enchanter (HoS) and grandmaster (B&W).

Special swords:
- Iris (steel, HoS): Dealing damage with attacks charges the sword with energy. When attacking
  with the fully charged sword, weapon damage is increased by 5% of the attacker's Vitality
  (if the attack is successful, Vitality is drained). A strong attack with the fully charged
  sword spawns a phantom copy of the sword that deals additional damage, which goes through
  shields, blocks and Armor and ignores damage resistances. Hits to fully charge the sword: 4.
  Charges decay over time (one per 6 sec.) and reset when Geralt takes damage. Fully charged
  sword discharges in 6 sec. or after a strong attack.
- Aerondight (silver, B&W): While in combat, weapon damage increases by 10 points per enemy.
  Each successful hit charges the sword. Fully charged sword discharges on successful hit,
  dealing critical damage with 100% Attack Power bonus and ignoring Armor and damage
  resistances. Sword discharge also damages and staggers all the surrounding enemies.
  Hits to fully charge the sword: 4. Charges decay over time (one per 6 sec.) and reset when
  Geralt takes damage.

========

You can how have a decent crossbow build, if you invest into crossbow skills. If not, it's

- Damage from player's own bolts is no longer halved.

	Power). Yrden: slowdown effect is applied for 5 sec. Quen: heals Geralt for 50% of damage
	dealt. Axii: confusion effect is applied for 5 sec.
  * Invigoration: When at maximum Vitality, additional Vitality regeneration increases your
    weapon damage by 2% per 1% of excess Vitality gained (up to +50%). Works only in combat.
	Resets on damage taken and damage dealt.
  * Prolongation: Excess Adrenaline generated slows down potion expiration timer by 0.5% per
    1% of excess Adrenaline (3 points = 100%). Max bonus cannot exceed 50%. The bonus resets
	when you lose Adrenaline and at the end of the combat.

Glyphwords:
- Level 1:
  * Deflection: Armor deflects all arrows.
  * Heft: All damaging melee actions (except for special attacks) cost 20% more Stamina, but
    deal 20% more damage.
  * Depletion: Hitting enemies with Aard reduces their Stamina by 50%, which also increases
    knockdown and heavy knockdown chances.
  * Ignition: Enemies set alight with Igni have a 50% chance to ignite other enemies within a
    2 yard radius.
  * Beguilement: Each enemy affected by Axii Confusion adds 25% to your critical hit chance
    and critical hit power (up to 100%).
- Level 2:
  * Balance: Casting a Sign costs 20% less Stamina, but all melee actions (except for special
    attacks) cost 20% more Stamina.
  * Rotation: Igni's basic attack strikes all opponents in a 360-degree radius and deals 150
    more damage (scales with Sign Intensity). Burning chance is decreased by 33%.
  * Usurpation: Each enemy controlled by Axii Puppet adds 25% to your Attack Power and Sign
    Intensity (up to 100%).
  * Entanglement: When Yrden Magic Turret hits an enemy, an Yrden Magic Trap is placed at that
    position. Size, duration and slowdown for such a trap are unaffected by Yrden Intensity and
    upgrades and limited to 2.5 yards, 5 sec and 35% respectively.
  * Protection: When you enter combat, you will automatically get a Quen shield without using
    any Stamina and with 100% bonus to Sign Power.
- Level 3:
  * Levity: All melee actions (except for special attacks) cost 20% less Stamina, but all
    Signs cost 20% more Stamina.
  * Retribution: Damage dealt by Quen Discharge and Exploding Shield is increased by 100%.
  * Possession: When an opponent influenced by Axii dies, the effect transfers to a nearby
    target. The effect's duration increases by 2 seconds for each blow the affected target
	lands.
  * Eruption: Foes killed by Igni (burning or fire) explode dealing 300 damage to other
    enemies within a 3-yard radius and igniting them. Damage scales with Sign Intensity.

Runes:
- Pierog rune gives +5 normal vitality regen and Tvarog rune gives +5 combat vitality regen
  (was no bonus).
- Glyph of Mending regen buffed to 3/4/5.
- Glyph of Warding bonus changed to Quen chance on projectile: 3/4/5%.

Mutations
=========

- Magic Sensibilities: Signs can deliver Critical Hits. Critical chance and damage scale with
  Sign Intensity. Opponents killed by critical hits from Signs explode. [Starting crit chance
  and bonus (SI=0) are now 5% and 25% respectively. They can go up to 85% and 400% at SI=500%
  - sign intensity to power bonus conversion formula is the same as for all the other sign
  effects in the mod.]
- Deadly Counter: Countering an enemy when their health is at 15% or less kills them and
  triggers a finisher or dismemberment. Each adrenaline point increases the damage dealt when
  countering by 33% (up to +100% max).
- Toxic Blood: Turns the blood of the witcher into a highly caustic acid. Toxicity Decay Rate
  is decreased by 50%. Whenever you take melee damage, your blood causes the attacker to lose
  1.5 point(s) of their health for every 1 point in your Toxicity level per second. The effect
  lasts 5 sec. Slows down the attacker's stamina regeneration by 10%.
- Piercing Cold: Aard gains a 30% chance to freeze opponents, and if the blast also knocks them
  down it will kill them instantly. Enemies who escape the freezing take 250 damage instead
  (scales with Sign Intensity).
- Bloodbath: Each fatal blow of your sword triggers a finisher or dismemberment. Upon entering
  combat you immediately get a bonus to your Attack Power for each skill equipped: +10% for a
  combat skill, +5% for any other skill. Getting hit decreases the bonus by 0.5% per 1% of
  health lost. Killing an enemy fully restores the bonus and adds +20% more on top of it. Max
  bonus: 250%. Resets at the end of the combat.
- Euphoria: Increases Vitality drain on overdosing by 0.75% for every 1 point in your Toxicity
  level. The amount of Vitality you lose when overdosing is added to your base weapon and Sign
  damage.
- Mutated Skin: When you are about to take damage your skin absorbs and redistributes that
  damage over time instead: 5 sec per each Adrenaline Point you have (up to 15 sec max, at
  least 1 point is required to trigger the mutation). During this period your Adrenaline is
  drained at a speed of 1 pt per 5 sec. If your Adrenaline reaches zero before the effect ends,
  all the remaining damage will be dealt at once.
- Cat Eyes: Crossbow bolts deal 100% more base damage and will pierce and either stun or knock
  down opponents. Opponents at full health lose 15% of their health when shot. If at non-zero
  toxicity, time slows down by additional 25% when aiming with a crossbow.
- Conductors of Magic: When drawn, magic, relic and witcher swords increase base Sign damage by
  30% of their own base damage.
- Adrenaline Rush: While in combat you alternate between two states: Rush state that doubles
  your Adrenaline gain for 30 sec and gives additional +50% to Attack Power and Sign Intensity
  per Adrenaline Point and Recovery state that halves your Adrenaline gain for 30 sec and gives
  -50% to the same characteristics for each Adrenaline point gained during the Rush state.
- Metamorphosis: Allows to imbibe up to 3 decoctions with no toxicity cost. Each free decoction
  imbibed increases your chances to apply critical effects with both swords and Signs by 10%
  (effect chance increases by 1.3 times max). Witcher Senses help you see better in dark places.
- Second Life -> Life Leech: Requires Mutation Catalyst potion to activate. Drains the life out
  of up to three surrounding enemies healing the witcher in the process. Drain rate is a sum of
  your weapon damage, Attack Power and Sign Intensity multiplied by Adrenaline Points and
  divided by the number of enemies. The effect lasts for 6 sec and can only be activated once
  per 90 sec.
- Mutation Catalyst: This potion serves as a catalyst to activate the Life Leech mutation.
  You get the recipe after researching Life Leech. If you load a save with Life Leech already
  researched, the recipe is added automatically.
  (Behaves as a regular potion and is a subject to all the corresponding buffs for potions you
  might have.)

Experience
==========

- Experience tables were reworked, difficulty dependent modifiers were removed. Experience
  scaling is now smooth: you get progressively less experience for lower level quests and
  more experience for higher level quests.
- Experience for kills was reworked, you get progressively less experience for killing
  monsters of the same type.
- You always get at least 5 XP for quests and at least 1 XP for monsters.
- Smooth experience adjustment means that you can can now get more experience for higher
  level quests, so they're now worth the trouble.
- Quest levels were tweaked so they make more sense (especially for Novigrad and Skellige
  where completing level 14 quest could result in next quest being level 9).
- Some of the side quests related to main quest were moved later into the game, because they
  can indeed be completed later and to spread experience and quests a bit.

AI
==

- Some of the bestiary entries tweaked to better reflect weaknesses of different monsters.
- Melee hit reaction severity reduction implemented for huge enemies (kind of a poise system
  for NPCs): the bigger they are, the less severe their hit reaction is.
- Golems are now immune to hit stagger, i.e. getting hit won't interrupt their actions.
- Monster hunt monsters are now immune to instant kill moves.
- Individual monster armor and resistances changed significantly, armored monsters are indeed
  armored and you need to deal with it. Armor penetration bonuses are now useful.
- Removed 87.5% of fire resistance from humans.
- Added different resistances to human armor.
- Damage values for NPC weapons tweaked (group enemies deal less damage in general).
- Health values for NPCs tweaked: group enemies have less health, contract monsters and bosses
  have more health.
- Levelup bonuses for NPCs redone: NPCs get slight flat increase in both health and damage and
  also percentage increase in health and damage. As a result, both armor and resistances are
  defined by enemy type alone, so you always deal full damage to enemies with zero armor and
  zero resistances regardless of their level and your weapon level. And when enemies level up,
  they won't end up having roughly the same health and dealing roughly the same damage as it
  was before: different enemy types will maintain their health and damage differences.
- Bonuses for deadly (aka "red skull") enemies were removed.
- Removed ??? from deadly enemies, now they just show their level normally.
- AI combat parameters were tweaked to make them react better to player actions.
- Stamina is now properly checked and drained for all types of attack and defense actions.
- AI should be better at dodging.
- Adjusted AI targeting priorities, so they will now attack enemies who are dealing damage to
  them first and will not prioritize the player that much - makes Axii Puppet more useful.
- Changed how damage is calculated for allied NPCs: both dealt and received.
- Cyclops no longer ignore Axii Confusion effect.
- Killing a frozen rotfiend now prevents the explosion. Freezing a rotfiend that is about to
  explode also stops the explosion.
- Wild Hunt minions, warriors and bosses now deal additional frost damage. You can counter
  this damage partially with better armor, monster hunt trophy (+50% resistance to frost) or
  with better dodging.
- Wild Hunt ice armor mechanic changed: it now adds 30% to all resistances instead of halving
  all damages. This means it can now be countered with resistance reduction and Igni is no
  longer mandatory on higher difficulties.
- Wild Hunt ice armor can now be removed by any fire damage and not just by Igni hit.
- Djinn rage and weakened bonuses tweaked: instead of +100%/-100% to stamina regen/sec,
  they're now +10%/-10% to stamina regen and +50%/-50% to attack power.
- Added -15% to damage resistances to weakened state (special enemies like Olgierd when you
  counter their special attacks or do some specific things).
- Caretaker's healing rate now depends on how much damage he deals, i.e. you can tank him
  with heavy build now. He still gets full heal from killing shades, though.

Difficulty modes
================

- Removed vanilla difficulty level restrictions for Ciri (the game is always easy or normal
  for her in vanilla). Ciri health, armor and damage tweaked accordingly.
- Experience gained no longer depends on difficulty level.
- Stamina regen for AI no longer boosted for DM.
- Difficulties:
  * Easy (Story): -50% to enemy health, -25% to enemy damage.
  * Normal (Sword and Story): no modifiers.
  * Hard (Blood and Broken Bones): +100% to enemy health, +100% to enemy damage.
  * Hardcore (Death Match): +150% to enemy health, +250% to enemy damage.
- New harcore difficulty preset added to GM options: increases the number of bonuses enemies
  get per level, makes mid to end game enemies tougher. Can be used with any base game
  difficulty mode.
- Note that vanilla scaling option is irrelevant with the mod.

UI
==

- Character stats menu was altered to show detailed sign infos. Common sign power now shows
  only base value and individual sign power stats show sign power with all modifiers. All
  damages, durations and chances are raw values, when applied to actual target they can be
  reduced to zero by target's resistance!
- Sword stats now show average damage per hit and not theoretical damage per second.
- Attack power and critical hit power stats were added to sword damage sections.
- Adrenaline gain stat was added to miscellaneous section of character stats.
- More stats were added for Toxicity section of character stats.
- Stamina section of character stats menu now shows stamina costs for all actions.
- Almost all skill descriptions were altered to show more data.
- Kill count statistic is now available from bestiary menu.
- Changed some of the loading screen tips and tutorials to reflect GM's changes.
- Extended recipe/schematic descriptions should be structured better now ('E' when buying
  an item).

Keep in mind that main damage related bonuses from swords (attack power, crit chance, crit
power, effects) are shown when the sword is sheathed, but all the other bonuses (like sign
power) are only shown when the sword is unsheathed.

Mod options
===========

- Vanilla scaling option is irrelevant with the mod.
- Default settings for Gameplay are now automatically loaded if you haven't set them up in
  the menu manually (with presets or sliders). If you're updating from the previous mod
  version, make sure to re-apply your personal Gameplay settings after loading a save.
- Enemy scaling presets: recommended - enables soft upscaling for low level enemies (-6
  levels from Geralt); hardcore - enables upscaling (i.e. all the low level enemies will be
  upscaled to match Geralt's level, higher level enemies won't change), adds more health and
  damage and enables progressive scaling for enemies; off - no scaling.
- Enemy scaling:
    * None - off, default levels are used.
	* Upscale - low level enemies are upscaled to match Geralt's level, high level enemies
	  use default levels.
	* Match - all enemies are upscaled/downscaled to match Geralt's level.
	* Soft upscaling - enemies that are 7+ levels below Geralt are upscaled to be exactly 6
	  levels below Geralt.
	* Random - default levels are ignored, enemies are assigned a random level based on
	  min/max boundaries set (see below).
	* Random no downscaling - low level enemies are assigned a random level based on min/max
	  boundaries set (see below), high level enemies use default levels.
- Random scaling (lower bound) relative to player level: used to define min level value for
  Random/Random no downscaling enemy scaling option (see above).
- Random scaling (upper bound) relative to player level: used to define max level value for
  Random/Random no downscaling enemy scaling option (see above).
- Use progressive level bonuses: enables progressive levelups for enemies. This means that
  instead of 1 bonus ability per 1 level enemies get more abilities per level as the game
  progresses.
- Level delta (LD): used with progressive levelups.
- Abilities per level delta (AD): used with progressive levelups.
- Progressive levelup formula: if current enemy level is lower than LD, nothing happens; else
  numberOfAbilities = (1 + AD/LD) * currentLevel - AD.
- Basically, LD and AD define the linear function slope for calculating the number of bonus
  abilities per level. (Bonus abilities define health and damage bonuses for NPCs per level).
- No additional levels for guards: disable additional levels for guards.
- Do not upscale animals: disable upscaling for animals.
- Enemy health/damage modifier: additional health and damage modifiers for enemies.
- Additional health/damage modifiers for bossed.
- Final health/damage formula: HP*(1 + difficultyModifier + enemyModifier + bossModifier).
  I.e. modifiers are additive, bosses use both generic enemy modifier and boss modifier.
- Experience presets: default - everything zero and off.
- Disable quest levels: disables levels for quests, affects the numbers displayed in UI and
  quest XP gain; doesn't affect NPC levels are those are independent from quest levels
  internally.
- Fixed experience for quests and combat: disables smooth experience scaling and progressive
  experience for kills, the amount of exp rewarded is always the same and is define by quest
  settings and monster type.
- Quest/combat experience modifiers: allow to reduce or increase the amount of exp gained
  from quests/combat. The formula: XP*(1 + modifier).
- Gameplay presets: recommended - enables boat sinking if overencumbered and cooldown for
  signs and special sword attacks; vanilla(ish) - restores stamina management to vanilla(ish)
  logic, i.e. melee has zero cost, signs drain 100% stamina; off - all zero and off.
- Sink the boat if overencumbered: enables boat sinking if Geralt is overencumbered.
- Overencumbrance threshold to sink the boat (%): defines overencumbrance threshold in
  percents relative to max carry weight at which boat sinking happens.
- Reduce item encumbrance (%): reduces item weight for all the items in percents.
- Enable stamina regen penalty for armors: enables vanilla logic for stamina regen penalty.
- Combat/Out of combat stamina regen (additional): allows to additionally increase/decrease
  stamina regen in/out of combat (relative to default values).
- Combat/Out of combat vitality regen (additional): allows to additionally increase/decrease
  vitality regen in/out of combat (relative to default values).
- Sprint/Jump/Melee/Sign action cost modifiers: stamina cost modifiers for corresponding
  actions. The formula: cost*(1 + modifier).
- Stamina regen delay (additional): allows to additionally increase/decrease stamina regen
  delay (relative to the default value).
- Melee special cooldown (seconds): cooldown time for Whirl and Rend skills.
- Sign cooldown (seconds): cooldown time for Signs.
- Alternate sign cooldown (seconds): cooldown time for alternative Sign casting modes.
- QoL presets: recommended - enables sign instant casting and low stamina sfx; off - turns
  off sign instant casting and low stamina sfx.
- Instant sign casting with select key: allows to instantly cast a sign when corresponding
  sign selection key is pressed (3-7 by default).
- Low stamina sfx threshold (percents): threshold stamina value at which low stamina sfx
  (heartbeat) starts playing.
- Low stamina sfx volume: heartbeat volume.
- Use dynamic low stamina sfx volume: heartbeat gets louder when stamina gets closer to
  zero.
- Low stamina sfx rate: heartbeat rate.
- Use dynamic low stamina sfx rate: heartbeat rate increases when stamina gets closer to
  zero.
